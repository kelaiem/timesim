well's radius, `min(SECONDS_WELL_R_ROUND, SECONDS_WELL_FORM_CEIL)` (TODO 298's form-rail ceiling), loosens with it.

the first fit lacks: §34 selector post az300 vs seconds-well ring: clearance -0.14"), so it belongs to the larger
fitted outline, not to the arc gong.
