## 302. case=free's dial hole ring crosses itself and its crystal carries zero-length curves — DONE

Filed 2026-11-13, out of TODO 300's local battery: the full native battery (`battery-native.mjs --query case=free
--shards 4`) runs 35/36. The failure is `outlines`, two rows, and main at f12ecaf8 reads both. The point tier, which
sweeps the free spec points, runs `inspection`, `clearances` and `undeclaredClearance` only, so this has never shown in
CI.

**What is wrong.**
- **A dial hole ring crosses itself.** It is an `ExtrudeGeometry` of the Dial unit, ring `hole`. On main it crosses at 3
  points, the first at (−36.04, −37.93), dial-local. On TODO 300's regenerated free outline it crosses at 8 points, the
  first at (−37.10, −35.83). It stands near a corner of the free outline, where a well held to the form outline would
  stand (§308 L4's wells outside the chapter). A self-crossing ring is not an outline: earcut fills it however it likes
  (§177's filled pivot bore).
- **The case crystal's outline carries zero-length curves.** `caseCrystal` has 128 curves shorter than the seam gap
  (TODO 273's rule) on main and 87 on TODO 300's branch, the first a `LineCurve` of length 0. The free outline's rings
  are `projectLoop` of its level loops onto the outer loop's vertices (form-case.js `formCaseLoops`), and several
  vertices project to one point, so the crystal's ring repeats points. The case's `offsetUnfolded` already drops such
  points for its fold test, but the crystal's prism is cut from the ring as is.

**Fix path.**
1. Name the dial hole: read which opening the ring is (seconds well, reserve well or sector, a foot) off the dial
   builder's inputs at that point, and why its outline folds there. For example, a well's circle clipped by the free
   outline at a re-entrant turn.
2. Cut the crystal (and any other ring built by `projectLoop`) from its loop with repeated points removed, the way
   `offsetUnfolded` reads it, and confirm the seams row empties.
3. Run `outlines` on every free spec point (`stopwork-140`, the strap rows) as well as `case=free`.

**Acceptance.** `outlines` passes on `case=free` and every free spec point, with its controls PASS.

**Done 2026-11-13.**
- **The dial ring was the applied chapter ring** (`makeDial`'s `loopRingExtrude`), whose inner edge was
  `offsetPolygon(outline, −width)`. That is a mitre offset of the dial's polyline, and it folds where the width passes the
  free outline's tightest concave turn. The ring now takes the EXACT offset of the shape the dial was cut from:
  `track.form` grown by `outlineGrow − width`, the free kind through its level-set offset (`freeOffset`). The mitre offset
  stays only for an outline with no shape.
- **The crystal's zero-length curves were repeated points** in a ring `projectLoop` builds for the free outline. `prism`
  (`form-case.js`) now drops consecutive repeats from a solid's outline. The rings themselves stay index-aligned for
  `boredBand`.

Measured, native battery `--only outlines,meshIntegrity`: PASS on `case=free`, on `stopwork-140`, on `case=rect` and on
`alarm=0&case=cushion`. The round dial has no outline and is untouched.
