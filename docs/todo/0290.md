## 290. The smaller round case's hammer head is cut short by the 45° frame land: restore the impedance match by position

Filed 2026-11-07, from §325 landing 12, which brought the round case in off its 40 mm cap (Ø38.22 mm) and let the gong
re-solve into the smaller annulus (ring r 48.19 → 45.85 u).

**What is wrong.** The alarm hammer's station follows the ring inward (pivot r 46.91 → 44.59, az 43.4° → 48.8°), and
at full draw the head now swings over the 45° quadrant's frame land (`pillarSeats[0]`, (28.74, 24.11), r 37.5,
`PILLAR_CAP_R`). Landing 12 made the clearance a law (`ALARM_HEAD_FRAME_PTS`, `alarmHeadFrameClear`, main.js). The head is the
longest whose drawn footprint stands `CLEAR_MARGIN` off every seat, and that length is **1.54 u** where the radial room
at rest is 2.33 (4.68 on the 40 mm build). The section is band-capped (6.60 u), so the head weighs **26.1 mg** against
the 45.6 mg modal mass it is meant to match: **μ 0.68**, the modelled level 52.27 dBA against 52.79 on the 40 mm build.
Every gate is green. The defect is P1, a match the strike group would design spent on a P3 conflict, which is the
direction the design-priority section forbids.

**Fix path, in position space.**
- **Re-site the land.** The 45° seat takes the nearest feasible bearing to its quadrant's ideal (`seatClearance`). The
  hammer's swept footprint is not one of its obstacles, because the frame is sited long before the alarm block. Hoisting the
  strike station's plan quantities (pivot, face, blow, draw) ahead of the frame, or giving the seat search a declared
  keep-out the alarm block then asserts, would let the land move off the swing. The land is structural, but it is
  the member with freedom here: its quadrant scan already ranges ±60°.
- **Or move the station**: the free end (`GONG_A1`) and the lever's siting decide the pivot. A station whose draw
  passes outboard of the land restores the radial room.

**Acceptance.** `ALARM_HEAD_DIMS.lOwner` reads "the radial room at rest" on the default build, the head's owner returns
to the impedance match (or the band, as at 40 mm), μ within 2% of the 40 mm build's, `plateSeats` clean, and the default
boots silent.
