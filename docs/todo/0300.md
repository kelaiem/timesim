## 300. A form case does not hold its movement: no clamp screw, no seat on the case middle's ledge, and the bore stands 2.3 u wide of the plate

Filed 2026-11-09, out of TODO 298 landing 2. That landing ran the whole native battery on form builds for the first
time. The battery's form point (`form-rect`) is swept by the point tier's three sweeps only, so the plate-seat tier
never read a form case, with or without the alarm.

**What is wrong.** On the round build the movement is located and held by the case. Its base plate's rim sits on the
case middle's ledge (`PLATE_SEATS` `Case / caseMiddle`, housed) and is fastened by the clamp screws through the rim's
bores (`Case / caseClampScrew`, fastened, §186). The rim's widest reach stands `SEAT_FIT` (0.08 u) inside the back band's
bore (§186's locating fit, re-measured at boot). On every form kind none of the three holds:

- **No clamp screw is cut.** The form case builds none (`form-case.js`), so `PLATE_SEATS`' `Case / caseClampScrew`
  row names no mesh (`plateSeats` malformed).
- **Nothing seats on the case middle.** `Case / caseMiddle` is never within `CLEAR_MARGIN` of the plate over §152's
  pose net (`plateSeats` stale: "the row buys silence for nothing").
- **The bore does not locate the plate.** §186's own boot warning on every form build: "the rim-to-bore fit measures
  2.3061 u against the SEAT_FIT 0.0800 it was derived to — the form case's bore (plate + CASE_CLEAR) does not locate the
  form plate".

Measured with the native battery (`battery-native.mjs --query`), on this branch's tree:
- `case=fitted` (the alarm built): `plateSeats` fails on exactly the two rows above.
- `alarm=0&case=fitted` at its own fit (TODO 298 landing 2): the same two.

**Why it has not been seen.** Every form spec point declares the §186 row as one of "the two rows every form build
carries" (§308 landing 5: "filed debt about the case, not a station out of its window"). Until now that debt had no
item and no fix path, and the plate-seat tier that would have measured it does not run on a spec point.

**What this does not establish.** Whether anything ELSE holds the movement in a form case: an axial press of the case
back on the back plate, or the stems through their tubes. The plate-seat tier reads only the base plate, and nothing
here reads the frame's other contacts with the case. That is the first thing a fix reads.

**Step 1, measured 2026-11-09: what the form case touches of the movement.** Every labelled unit's clearance to the
`Case` at the battery's rest pose (`inspect.js` `clearanceAt`), the round default against `case=fitted` (both with the
alarm):
- Round: the alarm crown and the pusher (the stems in their tubes) at 0.01; then the gong group at 0.15–0.16, and the
  three-quarter plate at 0.3202. The base plate, a held fixture and so not in this list, sits on the ledge.
- Fitted: the alarm crown and the pusher at 0.01. The three-quarter plate stands 1.8768 off, and nothing seats.

So a form case holds its movement by its STEMS alone: on an alarm-free form build, by the winding stem alone. The
round case's hold is a stepped bore (`geometry.js` makeCase, §186). The base plate carries a mounting RIM wider than the
plate, which drops into the back bore (`R_BORE_BACK`, the rim plus `SEAT_FIT`) and rests on a ledge at the rim's
underside (`zLedge`); clamp screws through the rim's bores thread into the ledge. A form plate has no rim (the rim's
clamp bores are among the openings §307 drops as standing outside the form outline), and the form case's bore is the
outline grown `CASE_CLEAR`, one wall all the way down (`form-case.js`).

**Step 2, the cost of each hold, 2026-11-10 (before any metal).** Two designs, worked through §186's own floors (`main.js`
4092–4215), in movement units (1 u = 0.379 mm) with the smallest clamp stock the round build already uses (Ø0.6 mm,
head 1.8:1, engagement 0.7 mm):
- **A — §186 carried whole, as a RING.** A mounting rim on the base plate's outline, a back bore one `SEAT_FIT` round
  it, a ledge and three clamps, every radius replaced by an offset from the plate's cut outline. The governing floor is
  the clamp thread's wall with the rim's land: CASE_CLEAR + D/4 + D/2 + land + (D/2 + fit) + 2·bevel + fit ≈ 2.639 +
  0.396 + 0.792 + 0.317 + 0.872 + 0.64 + 0.08 ≈ **5.74 u (2.17 mm)**, against today's single bore at `CASE_CLEAR`, 2.64 u.
  The back band would stand **3.1 u (1.17 mm) further out all round**. That is about **+150 mm² (+16%)** on the fitted
  case's ~132 mm perimeter, the reverse of §339's aim. Local tabs do not escape it: a 3.1 u pocket at each clamp station
  cuts through the 2.64 u band wall.
- **B — casing clamps on a FITTED bore, no rim.** The bore locates the plate's own outline at `SEAT_FIT`, not
  `CASE_CLEAR`, so the case comes IN by about 0.97 mm a side. The plate rests on a ledge, and screws in the band press
  dogs onto the plate's back face. This is how most form cases hold a movement. Its open problem is the ledge: the dial
  is cut to the same outline as the plate, so the ledge has to bear where the dial is not, or the dial is cut inside it.

**Fix path.**
1. Read what the form case's metal touches of the movement over the pose net, every frame member against every case
   member, to establish what (if anything) locates it.
2. Cut the form case's locating seat and its fastening, by §186's own rules carried to the shape:
   - **a mounting rim on the form base plate**: its outline grown by the round rim's width, cut to the shape;
   - **a stepped form bore**: the back bore at the rim's outline grown `SEAT_FIT`, a ledge at the rim's underside, and
     the front bore at the plate's outline grown `CASE_CLEAR`, as now;
   - **clamp screws** through the rim at stations its outline holds, threading into the ledge.
   The rim grows the counted metal, so every generated fit that reads it moves: the gong seats on every form kind
   (`tools/gong-seats-jobs.mjs`), the alarm-free fits (`tools/form-fits.mjs`) and the free outlines
   (`tools/free-outline.mjs`).
3. Retire §186's form-case row from every form point's `expectWarns` the day it stops firing (the spec-boot tier fails
   a declared row that no longer fires, so this is enforced).

**Acceptance.** On every form kind, with and without the alarm, `plateSeats` reads the case middle seated and the clamp
screws fastened, §186's fit lands on `SEAT_FIT` within its tenth, and no form point declares the §186 row.
