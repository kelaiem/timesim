## 305. stopwork-140's setting traverse runs through the keyless works' winding pinion — DONE

Filed 2026-11-13, out of TODO 304's sweep. On `stopwork-140` (`case=free&alarm=0&barrelstep=-140&rsvaz=0&rsvidl=2&
frame=lands&drumaz=140`) the native battery's `intraUnit` FAILS with three movers of the Keyless works inside its own
fixture `settingTraverse1` (tier MF):

> windingPinion ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `beat f=0`; windPinionSaw ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `beat f=0`;
> stemSquare ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `reserve f=0.25`

It reads identically before and after TODO 304's fix, so it is that arrangement's own. The arrangement is what does it:
`intraUnit` PASSES on `alarm=0&case=free` and on `case=free&alarm=0&frame=lands`, and FAILS on
`case=free&alarm=0&barrelstep=-140&drumaz=140`. The point tier sweeps only `inspection`, `clearances` and
`undeclaredClearance`, so no gate has seen it.

**What is wrong.** On this arrangement the stem stands on the other side of the movement. The winding pinion's box is
x 19.96–21.99, y 16.43–18.76, where `case=free&alarm=0` has it at x −26.35 to −25.10. `settingTraverse1`, the motion-works
fold's first leg (`MW_FOLD.len1`, `main.js` ~9954), now spans x −24.83 to 21.82, y 3.01 to 28.61, 46 u across the
movement. It stands in z −3.55 to −2.45, inside the winding pinion's z −5.59 to −2.82. Its default-arrangement span is
11 u. So the fold reaches across to the moved stem through the winding train's band, and the pinion, its saw and the stem
square turn through it.

**Fix path** (the traverse is a LINK: P3's "a link yields"):
1. Read `MW_FOLD`'s solve on this arrangement: what it routes the setting leg to, and why it accepts a leg that crosses
   the keyless works' own movers.
2. Hold the leg off the keyless works' swept movers (the winding pinion, its saw, the stem square) by `CLEAR_MARGIN` in
   the fold's own obstacle set: re-route the link, or change its stratum, never the pinion.
3. `intraUnit` PASS on `stopwork-140` and on the arrangement alone, and the default build's fingerprint unchanged.

**Closed** (2026-11-14) by re-solving the fold, not by moving the stem.

The cause, in the stem's own frame (along, side): every station is the same on both builds except the keyless
cluster's side, `sideSign`. stopwork-140 is −1: A stands at +7.48 where the default's stands at −7.48. The motion works
and the barrel do not mirror. So on stopwork-140 the straight run A→B passes the barrel's column 4.9 off. The fold still
laid leg 2 tangent to the column (β2 = φ + asin(needCol/dB)), and that was ≤ 0 at every bearing: "the rays do not
meet". §325's walk then opened −103.87°, through the pinion. (Mirroring the held heading with `sideSign` was tried and
refused: with the motion works unmirrored it gave legs of 24 against the turning target's 19.8.)

What landed, in `src/main.js`:
1. **The stem**, in CAP_SOLVE's judge (above): a capsule of `KEYLESS_STACK_R` about the stem axis. The candidate's rods
   are judged as segments.
2. **K placed for its legs where the column does not bind** (`solveSettingFold`). Where βcol ≤ 0, the barrel stands
   across the run from K and any leg 2 swung toward K clears it. K then goes on leg 1's ray at the point minimising the
   worse leg's length over its turning target, which is what the fold exists for. Where βcol > 0, which includes the
   default, nothing changes.
3. **A cuttable corner**: K's deflection is at least `FOLD_DEFLECT_MIN` (14.50°). That is solved by bisection on Σ
   against makeConicalGear's 85° tip-cone floor, on both members' bores.
4. **A borne tail**: a candidate whose rise, stub or cap arbor would run unborne is refused, as one whose tail no bearing
   fits is. The scan otherwise took the first bearing that opened, and the shipped build ran the rise unborne.
5. **A one-degree step** (`FOLD_SEEN_WALK_DEG`, measured), keeping leg 2's K block, before that block is dropped. It runs
   only where the held heading refused a fold for its tail. stopwork-140's held heading gives 145 folds and none opens
   borne; −1° (−152.87°) opens at bearing 29.25° with every bush.
6. **The battery's tie control** (`undeclaredClearance`, `inspect.js`) reads `clock.settingPinch`, the build's word on
   whether leg 2 passes over `reservePinion0`, where `MW_LEG2_R` is cut to stand exactly `CLEAR_MARGIN` off it. It holds
   the word both ways: an exact tie where leg 2 crosses, clear past the bound where it does not. A build with no word is
   held to the tie.
