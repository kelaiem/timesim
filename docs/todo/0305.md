## 305. stopwork-140's setting traverse runs through the keyless works' winding pinion

Filed 2026-11-13, out of TODO 304's sweep. On `stopwork-140` (`case=free&alarm=0&barrelstep=-140&rsvaz=0&rsvidl=2&
frame=lands&drumaz=140`) the native battery's `intraUnit` FAILS with three movers of the Keyless works inside its own
fixture `settingTraverse1` (tier MF):

> windingPinion ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `beat f=0`; windPinionSaw ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `beat f=0`;
> stemSquare ⇄ settingTraverse1 at `reserve f=0.25`

It reads identically before and after TODO 304's fix, so it is that arrangement's own. The arrangement is what does it:
`intraUnit` PASSES on `alarm=0&case=free` and on `case=free&alarm=0&frame=lands`, and FAILS on
`case=free&alarm=0&barrelstep=-140&drumaz=140`. The point tier sweeps only `inspection`, `clearances` and
`undeclaredClearance`, so no gate has seen it.

**What is wrong.** On this arrangement the stem stands on the other side of the movement. The winding pinion's box is
x 19.96–21.99, y 16.43–18.76, where `case=free&alarm=0` has it at x −26.35 to −25.10. `settingTraverse1`, the motion-works
fold's first leg (`MW_FOLD.len1`, `main.js` ~9954), now spans x −24.83 to 21.82, y 3.01 to 28.61, 46 u across the
movement. It stands in z −3.55 to −2.45, inside the winding pinion's z −5.59 to −2.82. Its default-arrangement span is
11 u. So the fold reaches across to the moved stem through the winding train's band, and the pinion, its saw and the stem
square turn through it.

**Fix path** (the traverse is a LINK: P3's "a link yields"):
1. Read `MW_FOLD`'s solve on this arrangement: what it routes the setting leg to, and why it accepts a leg that crosses
   the keyless works' own movers.
2. Hold the leg off the keyless works' swept movers (the winding pinion, its saw, the stem square) by `CLEAR_MARGIN` in
   the fold's own obstacle set: re-route the link, or change its stratum, never the pinion.
3. `intraUnit` PASS on `stopwork-140` and on the arrangement alone, and the default build's fingerprint unchanged.

**Acceptance.** `intraUnit` passes on `stopwork-140` with no new waiver. That, with TODO 304's clean point sweep, lets the
point be listed in `src/validated-configs.js` (`node tools/validated-configs.mjs --write --points FILE`).

**In progress** (2026-11-14). The description above came from a boot that dropped part of the point's spec. On the point
itself, leg 1 runs from (22.25, 27.62) to K (17.97, 10.26) and the winding pinion stands on that line at about
(21, 17.6). They meet at rest (`meshClearance` 0, and 0.014 to the stem square). The fold always crosses the stem line,
because the setting arbor and the motion works stand on opposite sides of it: the default crosses with leg 2, about
16.7 along the stem, inboard of the stack (from about 27). On this point §325's heading walk opened at −103.87°, which
crosses at about 27, through the pinion, because CAP_SOLVE's judge reads nothing on the stem axis.

CAP_SOLVE now carries a `stem` clause: the stack is a capsule about the stem axis, of radius `KEYLESS_STACK_R` (TODO 211's
bound), from the stack's inboard end read off the metal out past the plate. The candidate's rods are judged as
segments, every other mesh by its points. The default build's fingerprint is unchanged (4020135707).

**It does not land alone.** With the stem in view, none of the walk's 180 headings opens a fold on stopwork-140. Every
heading past the fold's own clauses (−97.9° to −113.9°) is refused by the stem, the cap leg's corners or the transfer
arbor. The build then falls back to an unbuildable corner: the bevel's cone distance comes out negative and no blank is
left. That fallback warns rows the point does not declare and boots 117 s. So this is a LAYOUT problem (P3). The
candidates are:
- a stratum change, leg 1 dipping under the stack through a pair of corners, each a real part with its P1 duties;
- re-solving the setting arbor's side, or the cap bearing's range, on arranged trains, so the crossing falls inboard of
  the stack as it does on the default.

**The stratum change, measured and refused** (2026-11-14). Above the stack there is no room: the stack's top stands
CLEAR_MARGIN under the plate face by design (`Z_KEYLESS`). Below it, the band from the stack's bottom (−5.958) to the
dial's back (about −7.95, `Z_DIAL` −8.48 on the no-alarm build) is tall enough in z for a dipped leg 1 (two radii of
0.55 plus the margins, 1.4). In plan it is full. On stopwork-140, every mesh vertex within ±3.5 of the stem line in
z −7.9..−5.85 was binned by its station along the stem:
- 20–27: the crown wheel, and at 22–24 the transfer arbor and the crown wheel's screw;
- 27–30: the winding pinion's saw, the clutch, the yoke's body and prong;
- 31: no vertex sampled, a 1 u window against the 1.4 a dipped leg needs (and a vertex read can miss a face);
- 32–39: the setting wheel, its bevel, the setting arbor and the minute wheel;
- 40: one unnamed Keyless works mesh.

The keyless works fills the band under its own stack, so a leg dipped beneath the stem only trades the pinion for the
crown wheel, the yoke or the setting wheel. What remains is re-solving where the fold crosses the stem line on an
arranged train (the setting arbor's side, or the cap bearing's range), so the crossing falls inboard of the stack as it
does on the default.
