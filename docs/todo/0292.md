## 292. The 28,800 and 36,000 A/h rows have no balance the honest mainspring can drive to the target

Filed 2026-11-08, from TODO 291, which proportioned the going mainspring to the trade's packing rule and so cut its
stored energy to 54.5% of the old ribbon's.

**What is wrong.** Every rate row carries a balance solved the TODO 207 / TODO 242 way: the heaviest rim on
`BAL_RIM_STEP`'s grid that sustains `AMPLITUDE_TARGET_DEG` (200°) at the nominal corner hanging and starts itself, on
the largest wheel (`BAL_R_STEP`) that has one. Under the new spring the 18,000 and 21,600 rows re-solve (rims 0.36 and
0.26 on the r 9 wheel). The two fast rows do not. Measured with the record's own step test (`equalisation`'s
`amplitude.target`, read off booted builds with each row patched):

| rate | wheel r | rim (the grid's lightest, 0.235) | nominal vertical | starts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28,800 | 9 | 0.235 | 144.75° | no |
| 28,800 | 8.75 | 0.235 | 150.44° | no |
| 36,000 | 7.5 | 0.235 | 138.95° | no |
| 36,000 | 7 | 0.235 | 152.89° | no |

A smaller wheel gains about 14° per half-unit at 36,000 and costs an escapement re-solve each step (the roller, the
escape-to-balance distance, the notch, bank and knock scale off `BALANCE_R`). None of these wheels starts itself, and
nothing measured says a smaller one would.

**What was done instead of shipping a stale balance.** The rows keep their gear trains (§204's cut stands) and carry
`undriven` in place of a balance. `SPEC` refuses such a `?vph=` loudly (`TODO 292: ?vph=… is refused`) and builds the
18,000 watch. `SPEC_RATES`, which the rate menu renders, lists only driven rows, and `SPEC_RATES_UNDRIVEN` the others.
`tools/probe-204-rates.mjs` holds the refusal: each undriven rate boots the 18,000 watch, names this item, and is not
offered. §204's old claim, that the 36,000 row trips the same asserts as the 28,800 row, compared two rows that no longer
build, and it was retired with them.

**Fix path.** Restore the energy, not the target.
- **More spring.** BACKLOG §339's second drum (a parallel twin) or a larger drum. Either raises the energy column with no
  change to the fusee, whose cone is cut against the torque law normalised to its wind angles.
- **Or re-solve the fast rows' oscillators whole**: smaller wheels, each with its escapement re-solved, searched until
  one both reaches the target and starts. That search is long, and it may end by measuring that no wheel can.

**Acceptance.** Each fast row carries a balance again, solved the TODO 242 way (`equalisation` row 15 green at its own
`?vph=`). Its `undriven` is deleted, and `probe-204-rates.mjs` reports it among the driven rows.
