## 292. The 28,800 and 36,000 A/h rows have no balance the honest mainspring can drive to the target

Filed 2026-11-08, from TODO 291, which proportioned the going mainspring to the trade's packing rule and so cut its
stored energy to 54.5% of the old ribbon's.

**What is wrong.** Every rate row carries a balance solved the TODO 207 / TODO 242 way: the heaviest rim on
`BAL_RIM_STEP`'s grid that sustains `AMPLITUDE_TARGET_DEG` (200°) at the nominal corner hanging and starts itself, on
the largest wheel (`BAL_R_STEP`) that has one. Under the new spring the 18,000 and 21,600 rows re-solve (rims 0.36 and
0.26 on the r 9 wheel). The two fast rows do not. Measured with the record's own step test (`equalisation`'s
`amplitude.target`, read off booted builds with each row patched):

| rate | wheel r | rim (the grid's lightest, 0.235) | nominal vertical | starts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28,800 | 9 | 0.235 | 144.75° | no |
| 28,800 | 8.75 | 0.235 | 150.44° | no |
| 36,000 | 7.5 | 0.235 | 138.95° | no |
| 36,000 | 7 | 0.235 | 152.89° | no |

A smaller wheel gains about 14° per half-unit at 36,000 and costs an escapement re-solve each step (the roller, the
escape-to-balance distance, the notch, bank and knock scale off `BALANCE_R`). None of these wheels starts itself, and
nothing measured says a smaller one would.

**What was done instead of shipping a stale balance.** The rows keep their gear trains (§204's cut stands) and carry
`undriven` in place of a balance. `SPEC` refuses such a `?vph=` loudly (`TODO 292: ?vph=… is refused`) and builds the
18,000 watch. `SPEC_RATES`, which the rate menu renders, lists only driven rows, and `SPEC_RATES_UNDRIVEN` the others.
`tools/probe-204-rates.mjs` holds the refusal: each undriven rate boots the 18,000 watch, names this item, and is not
offered. §204's old claim, that the 36,000 row trips the same asserts as the 28,800 row, compared two rows that no longer
build, and it was retired with them.

**Fix path.** Restore the energy, not the target.
- **More spring.** BACKLOG §339's second drum (a parallel twin) or a larger drum. Either raises the energy column with no
  change to the fusee, whose cone is cut against the torque law normalised to its wind angles.
- **Or re-solve the fast rows' oscillators whole**: smaller wheels, each with its escapement re-solved, searched until
  one both reaches the target and starts. That search is long, and it may end by measuring that no wheel can.

**Acceptance.** Each fast row carries a balance again, solved the TODO 242 way (`equalisation` row 15 green at its own
`?vph=`). Its `undriven` is deleted, and `probe-204-rates.mjs` reports it among the driven rows.

**Measured 2026-11-15: what stops the 36,000 row is the shake's CROSSING, not the balance**
(`tools/probe-292-fast-start.mjs`, a report, on `alarm=0` at main 2996635a, each boot the row patched to carry a balance at
the rim grid's lightest, 0.235). The balance's own model (`balanceDriveModel`) was read rather than `amplitude.target`,
because the alarm-free build publishes no EQUALISATION record (TODO 313). Controls:
- at 21,600 the probe reads the record's own 200.71° and its start;
- on the alarm build at 36,000, r 7 and r 7.5 read this item's 152.89° and 138.95° to the hundredth;
- every boot beats at 5 Hz.

**No wheel starts on today's spring, however small.**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nominal vertical | 243.52° | 213.45° | 189.25° | 169.41° | 152.89° | 138.95° | 127.05° | 103.85° |

r 6.5 and smaller fall out of the 0.02–0.04 mm stock. Each stays stuck with zero motion, because the escapement's torque
on the balance at rest is exactly 0.

**Why.** After the unlock the escape torque drives the fork across the shake, carrying the escape wheel and the train
reflected behind it (`FORK_CROSSING_SOLVE`), while the balance runs on at the posed swing. The impulse table is zero
until that crossing ends.

| | crossing time | balance turns meanwhile | impulse begins |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21,600 (r 9.25) | 2.49 ms | 9.38° | −5.81° (before centre) |
| 36,000, r 7.5 | 3.22 ms | 20.19° | +4.90° (past centre) |
| 36,000, r 9.25 | 3.45 ms | 21.67° | +6.48° (past centre) |

At 36,000 the escape torque is lower, because the train ratio is larger, and the balance moves twice as fast. So a
balance at rest stands where nothing pushes it. The fork geometry (`pinNotch`) is identical at both rates.

**What starts it** (diagnostic patches; nothing was re-cut):
- **The spring's released energy scaled at the same reserve (`POWER`).**
  - At ×1.5 nothing starts: the impulse still begins 0.8–1.3° past centre.
  - At ×1.75 r 7, 7.5 and 8 start (224.24°, 203.13° and 185.19°).
  - At ×2 every wheel r 5–8 starts, and r ≤ 8 reaches the target (r 8 201.93°, r 7 244.83°).
  - At ×3 every wheel starts.
  - The crossing's threshold is about **×1.67**, almost independent of the balance.
- **A second condition past it.** The first push from rest must carry the balance out of the lift (25°): escape torque
  against k = I·ω², so a lighter wheel helps.
  - At ×1.42 with the fork's and train's inertia ×0.6 (`CROSS_I`), r 7 starts (194.92°).
  - r 7.5 and r 8 do not; their first swings reach 22.7° and 19.0°.
  - At ×1.23 with the inertia ×0.5, r 7 reaches 24.7° and does not start.
- **The crossing alone, at today's energy (`CROSS_T`).** Doubling the torque that drives the crossing puts torque on the
  balance at rest (24.6× the pivots' friction), but r 7 then swings to only 20.9° and sticks.

**Two cautions this measurement adds:**
1. **The start test uses the crossing timed at the posed 200° swing, for a balance at rest.** `ESC_TORQUE`'s comment
   calls that conservative. A crossing paced from rest, which a real start is, may read a lower threshold than ×1.67.
2. **Every 36,000 boot carries warnings besides the start**, 2 (r 7.5) to 70 (r 9.25). Among them:
   - TODO 175's bore band on the 6-leaf escape pinion at m 0.168;
   - no footing for the fork cock's leg;
   - §125's D4 off the seconds well's maximum.
   
   So the row is not buildable as it stands, apart from the start.

**What this does to the fix path.** "Restore the energy" still holds, and the threshold is now measured: about ×1.7 of
today's spring at the same reserve, ×2 for the target on r ≤ 8. A smaller wheel alone cannot do it.
- The owner prioritised the parallel second barrel (BACKLOG §339's measured energy maximum, 224.5%).
- A lighter fork and escape train is the zero-footprint half of the same budget.

**The 36,000 row's fourth → escape mesh, measured 2026-11-15** (on `alarm=0&barrels=2&vph=36000`, §339 landing 3d's
build, each candidate patched into the row). The row cuts it 120/6 at m 0.168, so the fourth wheel keeps the 96-tooth
rows' pitch radius (§204). That is not metal on two counts:
- the 6-leaf pinion's root (0.271) falls inside its 0.4 bore, which `boreBandGuard` reports as TODO 175's bore band
  (0.3035 against 0.4);
- m 0.168 is under §50's module floor (`MODULE_MIN_U` 0.2016).

**No 6-leaf pinion clears the bore at any module** the fourth wheel can carry. Its root is 0.313 at the floor and 0.371
at m 0.25. The ratio is fixed at 20 (the seconds hand on the fourth, a 15-tooth escape wheel), so the candidates are
140/7 and 160/8. Each clears the bore band, and each moves the layout:

The maintaining detent's beak (TODO 218/221) and §329's lock disc fire in every candidate; they are the doubled drive's,
not the mesh's. So **140/7 at the module floor** is the mesh. Its fourth wheel is 4 u larger in radius, which moves the
escape and balance stations: a layout re-solve (the escape and balance steps, P3's position space) before the row
builds. The balance re-solves there too: the 7-leaf mesh's efficiency gives 203.00° on the declared r 8.25, and a
claim of 135.

**Built at §339 landing 3g, 2026-11-17.** The row is cut 140/7 at `module: 'floor'`, §50's `MODULE_MIN_U` (the law
moved into `layout.js` beside `STOCK_MIN_U`; `geared-geneva.js` re-exports it). The stations are re-laid.

- **What the default arrangement broke.** On the 140/7 build at the designed escape step (−57.9°), five layout warnings:
  the balance cock had no clear seat, the escape cock's foot and floor clashed, and the stop-work's pad and crank too.
- **The scan.** Escape step on a 1° grid, balance target 45–60°, each a native boot of `alarm=0&barrels=2&vph=36000`
  judged by its warnings, which carry `CLEAR_MARGIN`. Every step from −42° up boots clean at the designed balance
  target; −43° clamps the balance cock's reveal, and −60° and beyond fold the low corridor and the stop-work.
- **The choice.** The row's `parallel` entry declares `escapeStepDeg: -42`, the clean step nearest the design, and the
  balance keeps its designed target. The spec still overrides it.
- **The balance, re-solved on TODO 242's rule.**
  - Rim 0.240 sustains 200.75° and starts; 0.245 gives 198.56°. At the lightest rim, r 8.5 gives 194.42°.
  - The least sustained swing is 134.18°, so the claim is 134°.
- **What the build still raises.** Only §329's lock disc. The 7-leaf pinion took TODO 175's bore band with it.
