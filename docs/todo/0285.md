## 285. Measure reports the round plateR on every form case, no case figure, and the balance's faces as the depth — DONE

Filed and closed 2026-11-05. The owner asked whether the Measure overlay reports the case's size correctly under the form shapes.

**What was wrong.** Measure's figures all come from one derived set, `scaleReadout`, which §39's block builds at the end of the build. The stats line, the legend and §49's in-scene L-square all read that set. Three of its fields described something other than the metal:

- **The plan was `plateR · 2` on every build.** Under FORM (§307/§308/§325), `plateR` is deliberately kept for its other consumers (the case's radial chain, the tube standoff, the wells), while the plates and the case are cut to `FORM`. So every form build read `⌀31.5 mm`. Measured on the main tree:
  - rect plates are 34.65 × 33.64 mm;
  - the bare `?case=cushion|tonneau|fitted` guesses are 27.62 × 29.08 mm;
  - the free stopwork-140 outline is 29.19 × 29.38 mm.

  The L-square's height leaders stood at ±`plateR` too, 1.1 mm short of the rect plate's top and 1.2 mm past the cushion guess's. A form outline has no single diameter, so the figure should not have been one.
- **No case figure, on any build.** The set held the plate and the bare movement's depth (`movMM`). The cased depth was measured beside it (`casedMM`, published only to §261's height cap), and the case's plan was never read. So the overlay could not answer "how big is the watch". The answer is 40.0 mm across the body on the round build, and the form band's `outer` loop on a form one (the outline grown `CASE_CLEAR + CASE_BAND_T`).
- **The depth leaders marked the balance.** `zMinUnits`/`zMaxUnits` read `box`, which `unitBox('Balance')` had set two statements earlier. So the foot's leaders spanned −0.113..3.677 mm, the balance's faces, while the stats line quoted the assembly's 9.6 mm (−4.993..4.563).

**What was done.** The set now carries the plan the plates are actually cut to: FORM's bounds, or the ±`plateR` circle on the round build. It also carries the case's plan, read off the wall it is cut on (`CASE_R_OUT`, or `formCaseLoops(FORM).outer`, the loop `caseWallAlong` reads), and `casedMM`. The Z leaders read `movBox`.

The text quotes a plan as `⌀d` on the round build and `W × H` on a form one. It reuses the `Case`, `plate` and `deep` keys every locale already carries, so no new strings are added. The ruler's vertical leg spans the plan's own y bounds. On the round build its coordinates are unchanged: `[floor(MM(−R)), ceil(MM(R))]` is the old `±ceil(MM(R))`. The life-size caption's "mm across" reads the plan's width, which is the diameter on the round build. `__clock.scaleReadout` publishes the set.

**How it is held.** `tools/probe-measure-readout.mjs` is an acceptance test. It boots the round build, each box-fitted kind at its committed seat's fitted `formrect` (`src/gong-seats.js`), and the free stopwork-140 point. It turns Measure on through its button and holds the set to the metal:

- **M1, the plan, form builds.** The base plate slab's cap vertices must lie inside the plan and meet all four sides. A beveled extrude's outermost layer is the shape itself; its flanks stand 0.126 mm proud of it.
- **M1, the plan, round build.** The three-quarter plate must lie inside the plan and meet three of four sides. The base slab carries §186's mounting rim there (⌀37.9), and the plate's quarter is cut away for the balance.
- **M1, the ruler.** The ruler's y bounds must meet that metal.
- **M2, the case's plan.** It must equal the band mesh `caseMiddle`'s extent.
- **M3, the depths.** The cased depth and the leader faces must equal the metal's z extents.
- **M4, the text.** The stats line and the legend must quote the set, and a form build must quote no `⌀`.
- **C1, the control.** The old law must fail M1 on a form build.

All PASS on the six builds. Restoring the `box` read in a copy of the tree fails M3, as it should.

**Two traps it measured.** A bare `?case=<kind>` boots the solvers' first-boot guess, and the build warns that its metal stands 6 u outside it. Measured that way, a three-quarter plate ear stood 0.314 mm past the cushion's guess. That was the query's defect, not the readout's. The three-quarter plate is also no reference on a form build: it is cut in at its balance cutaway, and stands 0.052 mm off the tonneau's +x side.
