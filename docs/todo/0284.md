## 284. A form case picked with a geometry knob in the URL is cut to the stand-in box: the gong and the movement stand through the case

Filed 2026-11-05 from the owner's report on the development site:
`?cam=18.756,1.264,254.241&look=-13.91,-16.736,-16.804&schematic=0&focus=Balance+cock&position=DU&aes=dial.face.color~00c7d2&strap=18&case=fitted`
(the gong visibly through the fitted case).

**What is wrong.** A FITTED form case (§317) is cut to the shape `?formrect=` carries. With no `?formrect=` it is cut to
the solvers' stand-in box, `[-38.17, 34.71, -35.54, 41.20, 0.213651, 0]` on the default arrangement, which is much
smaller than the movement. The build knows it is wrong, and says so at boot, but builds through it:

- `§307/§308`: the movement's counted metal stands **−6.067 u** inside the outline it was cut to (need ≥ 5). The fit it
  needs is printed: `[-45.74, 45.74, -43.91, 44.38, 0.500767, 0.193092]` at a strap of 18 mm.
- `§310`: the bar gong stands **−6.585 u** inside the outline (need 1.601).
- And with them: two plate openings dropped as outside the form (§307), the hammer spring's stud overhanging the
  plate by 3.075 (§268 / TODO 267), the strike group's opening leaving −6.484 of plate (TODO 266), and the case sectors'
  annulus occupied by 4400 samples (§186). Ten boot warnings in all.

**It is not about the strap.** Measured locally with `tools/boot-form.mjs`, `case=fitted` with no `formrect` gives the
same −6.067 and −6.585 at 16, 18, 19 (the default) and 20 mm. With the fit the boot prints (or the gong seat's own,
`-45.72,45.72,-43.91,44.38,0.445876,0.162288`), the same builds boot with only the two declared debt rows (§186's rim fit
and §261's depth).

**How a reader reaches it.** `index.html`'s case picker (§310) takes the gong seat's fit only when the URL carries no
other geometry knob. Any other spec key, `strap` included, reloads with NO `formrect`, "for the boot to print its
formrect=". So the strap picker followed by the case picker, or a hand-typed `?case=fitted`, lands on the stand-in box. The
free case does not have this problem, because its outlines are a generated table per configuration
(`src/free-outlines.js`, every strap width included).

**Fix path, in order of preference.**
1. **Generate the fit, as the free case does.** A table of each fitted (and rect, cushion, tonneau) shape's `formrect`
   per configuration key (every strap the picker offers, the alarm off, the arrangements), written by a tool from a first
   boot's `__clock.formFootprint.rect` and checked by `--check`. The build reads it when the URL carries no `formrect`.
   The gong seats (`src/gong-seats.js`) are solved at those fits, so they move with it.
2. **At least, never build the stand-in silently wrong.** With no `formrect` and no generated row, re-boot once at the
   printed fit (the page already knows it), or carry the seat's fit when the only extra knob is one that does not move the
   movement's metal (the strap changes only the lug flats' need, and `strap=18` at its own printed fit builds clean).

**Acceptance.** `?strap=N&case=fitted` for every strap the picker offers, and `?case=K` for every form kind, boots with
only its declared debt rows: no §307/§308 metal-inside row and no §310 gong row. A swept spec point at a non-default strap
on the fitted case holds it.
