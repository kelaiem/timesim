## 278. Round ?alarm=0: the case step was set by the alarm stem, and the lug weld needs that band

Filed 2026-10-30, from §325 landing 3, which made `?alarm=0` build the movement without the
alarm complication.

**What is wrong.** On the ROUND case, `?alarm=0` boots with two warnings, one per lug pair:

    makeCase §280 lug: the weld's upper foot stands 3.7296 past the band's wall

`CASE_Z_STEP` (`src/main.js`, §186) is where the midcase steps from the thick back band
(`R_OUT`) to the front section: one `CLEAR_MARGIN` in front of the LOWEST crown bore, so both
crown sleeves live in the thick band. With the alarm built the lowest bore is the alarm crown's
(`alarmSpinner.position.z`), and the band that stem leaves is tall enough for each lug's welded
foot (`caseLugPlan`, `src/layout.js`, §280: the foot must stand one `STOCK_MIN_U` inside the
band's wall, `zWallLo = zStep`). Without the alarm the winding stem alone sets the step, the step
rises 3.73 u, and the foot no longer fits. Nothing else measured moves: the lug was sized
against a band the alarm's stem happened to provide.

**What it is not.** The free case (`?case=free&alarm=0`, the build §325 landing 3 is for) does
not warn: its lugs weld to the form case's flat ends (`caseLugPlan`'s `flat` path) and never read
the step. The default build does not move.

**Fix path.** The step is a consequence of two duties, not one: it must sit under the lowest
crown bore (§186) AND leave the lug's foot its sheet floor (§280). So
`CASE_Z_STEP = min(stems' rule, lugFootTop − STOCK_MIN_U)`, where `lugFootTop` is
`caseLugPlan`'s own `footTopZ`. It depends on the band's back face and the bar (`zMidBack`,
`z0`, set in `CASE_DIMS`) but not on the step, so it is not circular. In code order it is,
though: `CASE_SECTORS` reads `CASE_Z_STEP` before `CASE_DIMS` derives `zMidBack`. So the fix
moves the back-face derivation ahead of the sectors, or has `caseLugPlan` export the foot as a
function of `zWallHi`/`zBack` and evaluates it where both are known. At the default the
alarm stem's rule is the lower and the step does not move (the fingerprint holds that).
Acceptance: `?alarm=0` boots silent on the round case, the default's fingerprint is unchanged,
and the step's comment states both duties.
