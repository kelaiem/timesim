## 280. ?d4=16's leg-1 heading walk costs ~50 s of boot and times out the hosted spec boot

Filed 2026-11-03, alongside the fix that narrowed §325 landing 5's walk to its record's rule (`fix/cap-walk-trigger`).

**What is wrong.** From #842 (§325 landing 5) the hosted push battery failed every merge on `spec boots: every declared
spec point builds`: five points never produced a `__clock` inside `BOOT_TIMEOUT_MS` (120 s). They were alive — no boot
error, no page error, not wedged — and the self-hosted runner built all five with the same warning counts. That is why
#842's own PR (`[self-hosted]`) and every nightly stayed green while every hosted push went red.
`validated configs: every spec point's mark agrees with the set` failed beside it only because its control, `d4=16`, was
one of the five.

The cost was `CAP_SOLVE`'s heading walk (`src/main.js`). When the held heading opened no cap bearing, it re-ran the
whole ±60° bearing scan at every heading out to ±90°, up to 180 more scans. The fix that ships with this item walks
only where the held heading opens NO FOLD, the record's own rule ("held where it opens a fold"). That removes four of
the five: on `barrelstep=-20`, `rsvr=20`, `rsvr=30` and `reserveh=48&rsvr=27` the held heading gives folds (73 of 161
bearings past the fold's clauses) that the reserve and the cap leg refuse, and all 180 headings stayed shut. Boots fell
from ~130 s to 34–40 s here, and every fingerprint, heading and bearing is unchanged.

**`?d4=16` is what remains, and it is the walk doing its job.** At its held heading (−46.87°) no bearing gives a fold
(0 of 161), so it walks, and it opens at +48° (heading 1.13°, bearing −11.25°). That costs ~46–50 s of `CAP_SOLVE`:
96 headings, 444 candidates past the fold's clauses, 441 of them refused by the reserve. The boot runs ~67–87 s here
(Chrome 153, one to three boots at a time). Hosted runs about 1.4× this container (#841's green spec tier: 557 s for 39
points), so roughly 120 s, at the ceiling.

**Where the time goes, measured** (per candidate past the fold's clauses, ~70–100 ms):
- `solveCapLeg`, the cap leg's tilt solve: ~63–78 ms, about 58 judgements of φ each. Within it, rise ⇄ foot corners
  13.7 s, rise ⇄ motion-works discs 7.8 s, foot and cap ⇄ discs 5.8 s, `capLegAt` 4.8 s, the stub 2.1 s, over the
  whole walk.
- The phase-0 reserve swing solve on the refused candidates: 13.8 s, dominated by the 56k static points (`staticPts`)
  re-measured at all 121 swings for every candidate.
- The five corners, five rods and the cap pinion together: ~5 ms. Cutting metal is not the cost.

**Fix path.** Make the walk cheaper without changing any accepted answer (the walk's first opening heading, nearest
first, must stay the same, so the fingerprints of `d4=16` and the free −60°/−120°/−140° rows hold):
1. **Memoize what the heading does not reach.** The bearings B recur at every heading (`capAt(dl)` is
   heading-independent), and in `capLegAt`/`capLegCorners` the foot corner, the cap corner, the stub, E, D and Σ_E
   depend on (B, φ) alone. Only the rise corner reads `F.leg2U`. So the foot/cap ⇄ discs judgement and the
   (B, φ) part of `capLegAt` can be memoized exactly across headings. That is about a third of `solveCapLeg`.
2. **Compute the static obstacles' clearance once.** `solveReserveSwing(staticPts.concat(cand))` re-reads the static
   points per candidate. Their per-swing clearance is fixed for the whole solve, so a refusal-only gate needs only the
   candidate's points. Share `clearAt` between the two paths rather than writing the law twice.
3. **Gate on the metal known before `solveCapLeg`.** The legs and the drop and fold corners depend on F alone; a
   reserve refusal on a subset of the metal implies the full metal's (TODO 162's phase-0 argument). Measured: rods
   plus the fold corner catch 155 of `d4=16`'s 441 refusals. The foot corner catches 386 but needs φ.
Hold each against the identity of the walk's answer (`settingFold.leg1HeadingDeg`, `bearingDeg`) and the fingerprint on
`d4=16` and the free −60°/−120°/−140° rows, and against the spec tier on the hosted runner. A coarser walk step, derived
from the sampling law the other scans use, would be faster but can move which heading opens. That is a design choice
for those rows, not part of this fix.
