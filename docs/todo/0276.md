## 276. Alarm link tour captions are untranslated after the §261 retarget — CLOSED

Filed 2026-10-28, from PR #821, which re-aimed the "Trace" tour (`ALARM_LINK_STEPS`
in `src/main.js`, §37) at the chain §261 folded under the dial.

**What is wrong.** The retarget rewrote four captions and added one, so their
English keys no longer match any entry in the chrome's tables (`src/i18n.js`,
one table per `LOCALES` row). `t()` falls back to English VISIBLY, by design,
so a reader in any other locale now sees English on those stops while the
other three stops of the same tour stay translated. Nothing is broken and no
gate fails: `probe-chrome-coverage` was not run for that PR, and a table is
only measured when it is run.

The stops, by their new first words:

- "Between the plates the press runs through two 1:1 CRANKS…" (new stop 2)
- "Under the dial: one press = one index = half a column pitch. The link’s BEAK…" (was "One press = one index… A SECOND beak, 120° round…")
- "The beak lever is pivoted on a post beside the run…" (was "Its long TAIL carries that read across the south-west…")
- "The rod slides in a guide on the LAY SHAFT’s third hanger…" (was "Under the base plate, a LAY SHAFT… 32 units inboard…")

The stop that took the old "X-ray the plates: the beak’s fall drops the ROD
through bores in both…" is gone, so its entry in every table is orphaned.
The pusher, centre-crank and reverse-press captions are unchanged and still
translated.

**Closed 2026-10-28.** The four captions are translated in all twenty-three
tables and the four orphaned entries are gone (92 lines replaced, 23 × 4). The
gate the first draft of this item named was wrong: `probe-chrome-coverage` reads
the DOM at boot, and its own header lists a tour caption among the strings it
cannot see, which is why §261's retarget passed everything. The gate is
`tools/probe-277-route-i18n.mjs` since this item: it already held the Inspect
route's captions per locale (present, not an English echo, digits as a multiset,
`TODO n` ids, the modelled/simulated stems, each with a mutation control) and
now also reads `ALARM_LINK_STEPS`' seven captions out of `src/main.js`, so
rewriting one fails every locale loudly. Its first run found a real defect in
the new Japanese: it wrote "2 つ", "1 回", "3 ピッチ" where the English spells
the numbers out, which the digit-multiset rule forbids (the English carries
`1:1`, `§25`, `§3` and nothing else); they are 二つ, 一回, 三ピッチ, 三つ目 now.

**What stays open.** The translations are first-draft machine translations from
each locale's neighbouring captions, owed the same native review as any chrome
text (`node tools/l10n-review-packets.mjs`). Two terms had no neighbour to copy
and were coined per locale: *driver* (the part the last crank's pin drops into)
and, in the CJK, Arabic, Hebrew and Persian rows, *link* ("the link’s BEAK").
Those are the rows a native reader should look at first.
