## 317. The reversal detector's dwell floor is an angle, so a geared-down recoil reads as a dwell — DONE

Filed and fixed 2026-11-17, found on PR #999 (§339 3c–3f): `restoring` failed on `alarm=0&barrels=3&vph=36000`, on
`main` as well, with `staleDeclarations: ["Center wheel"]`.

**What was wrong.** `restoring`'s population is the §36 registry's `reversed` flag. For a revolving part the
registry decided it from the witness vertex's angle, step by step. A step under 1e-6 RAD was a dwell (no vote, no
reset), and a sign change between two voting steps was a reversal. Two faults, one root: an angle is the wrong
currency.

- **A recoil is divided down every mesh it travels back through.** The escape wheel's draw recoil, measured over two
  seconds of finely stepped poses, against the centre wheel's share of it:

  | build | escape wheel | centre wheel |
  |---|---|---|
  | default (14,400) | 9.1e-3 rad | 1.9e-5 rad |
  | 36,000 | 9.2e-3 rad | 7.7e-6 rad |

  The 36,000 train steps up 2.5× more between them. Each sampled step carries a fraction of the recoil, under the
  floor, so the wheel read as dwelling while its metal moved. `restoring` then called its true two-way declaration
  stale, because a part that does not reverse may not carry one.
- **A per-step floor goes blinder as the sampling gets finer**, and the registry's confirm pass re-samples finer.
  A reversal spread over many fine steps never votes at all.
- **The same blindness hid the motion works and the hour wheel on every build.** The recoil reaches them through the
  cannon pinion on the centre arbor, divided by 12, about 1.6e-6 rad at the hour wheel. They were never in the
  population, so nothing asked what drives them back.

**The fix.** `arcTrackReverses` (`src/inspect.js`) asks the METAL. It tracks the witness's unwrapped arc length (angle
× its radius about the fitted axis), and counts a reversal as a RETREAT from its running extreme by more than
`ARC_DWELL_U`, whatever the step size. The floor is one PLACE cell (`1 / DIGEST_PLACE_Q`, 1e-6 u), the digest's own bar
for "this metal did not move". The coarse pass and the confirm pass both call it, so the two cannot part.

- **Population:** the default's grows from 38 to 40 units. The motion works and the hour wheel are declared two-way in
  `main.js`, on the train's own terms: the mainspring forward, the escape wheel's recoil back.
- **36,000:** the centre wheel's declaration is no longer stale.

**Held by** `tools/probe-317-arc-reversal.mjs`:
- The law on synthetic tracks, each with the old law beside it as the control: a 3-floor recoil over 1000 steps
  reverses (the old law misses it); half a floor does not; float-noise jitter does not; a swing parked on its banking
  does; ten times the sampling reads the same.
- `restoring` on the 36,000 build (the train two-way, nothing stale) and on the default (the motion works and hour
  wheel declared, nothing unwaived).
