## 314. The geared stop-work's bulged fusee window leaves under TQ_LAND_MIN of plate to the fusee gears window at 15, 18 and 33 h

Filed 2026-11-15, out of §337 landing 3 (#995), which made the geared Geneva the default arrest at every reserve but 30 h.

**What is wrong.** On the geared build (the bare `?reserveh=N` since landing 3, `?arrest=geared` before it), §62's
window check fires twice at three reserves, at both of its stages (`re-cut` and `movement complete`):

| reserve | land between `fusee gears` and `fusee` | need (`TQ_LAND_MIN`) |
|---|---|---|
| 15 h | 1.808 | 1.900 |
| 18 h | 1.875 | 1.900 |
| 33 h | 1.866 | 1.900 |

The strip of three-quarter plate between the cone window (bulged for the geared stop-work) and the separate fusee gears
window is thinner than §50/§54 leave a plate member. The same reserves under `?arrest=stop-iron` carry no such row, and the
other nine geared reserves read clean, so the geared build ADDS these two rows at 15, 18 and 33 h and nothing else
(native boots of every reserve both ways, warnings compared with their numbers masked, 2026-11-15).

**Where it came from.** Not from landing 3: `main` alone, before #995 merged, boots the same two rows under
`?arrest=geared&reserveh=15` (1.808, identical). §347 (#989) shrank the round plate (the station term measured rather than
`PLATE_RIM_MARGIN`), the windows re-solved, and the geared site that cleared the gears window by its land before no longer
does. Landing 3's twelve-reserve comparison was measured before #989 merged and read clean at all twelve; re-measured on the
merged tree, these three moved. Landing 3 made those three builds the default for their reserves.

**What is known about the cause, and what is not.** The site search (`src/main.js`, §337 landing 2b's block, `room` /
`landAt`) holds every disc it widens the window by, and the branch arm's sampled path, a `TQ_LAND_MIN` off every other
window's edge (`tqEdgeLand(otherEdges)`). Measured at 15 h, the window's closest approach to `fusee gears` is NOT near any of
those: the near point is 6.5 or more from the cone wheel's centre, the finger, the star and both arm segments, against disc
radii of 3.0–4.6 and an arm half-width of 1.18. So the land is lost to something the search does not model. The suspect is
the cut's last step, `filletReflexCorners(unionLoops([...COCK_MERGE_PTS, FUSEE_COCK_SLOT, ...bulgePolys]), 2 * G.PLATE_BEVEL)`,
which rounds the union's inward corners and so takes plate where a disc meets the window as first cut. **Not confirmed**:
the next step is to read the outline at that point and see whether it is a fillet arc.

**Fix path.** P3, in position space (the mechanism's dimensions stay):
1. Confirm the cause by locating the near point on the cut outline (fillet arc, the offset polygon of a strip's corner, or
   the cock slot's own merge).
2. Make the search judge what the CUT leaves: build the candidate's outline the way the cut does (the same union, offset and
   fillet) and hold it a `TQ_LAND_MIN` off every other window, rejecting the candidate if it is not. The ranking (least
   bulge, then margin) is unchanged; a rejected best falls to the next arrangement.
3. Re-read all twelve reserves both ways (geared and `?arrest=stop-iron`) and restore the record's claim that the gear adds
   no row anywhere, or state where it still does.

**Why nothing caught it.** No spec point or probe boots 15, 18 or 33 h: the battery's geared points are 24 h and 36 h, and
`probe-337-geared-invariant.mjs` holds the branch arm and the cock's stall at all thirteen reserves, not the plate's windows.
A gate on "the geared build boots no warning its `?arrest=stop-iron` twin lacks" at every reserve would have; it would cost
24 boots, so it belongs with the invariant's own job if it is added.
