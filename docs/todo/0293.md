## 293. The maintaining spring's blade bears near its tip: the face runs 0.069 past the contact where the cut wanted a pin radius

Filed 2026-11-08, from TODO 291, whose honest mainspring lowered the going torque the maintaining spring is sized
against (TODO 219).

**What is wrong.** Under the lower torque (1.7603 N·mm, from 3.2296), `MAINT_SPRING` re-derived exactly as its rules
say. The blade got softer, so the most run the crossing allows grew. The ring took the fewest teeth whose pitch fits that
run (35 → 25), so the run grew from 0.224 to 0.297 rad, and the working deflection from 1.389 to 1.755 u. The solve
stands the blade's tip one `CLEAR_MARGIN` off the hub land in the CUT shape. As the hold relaxes the blade toward the
stop flank, the free end swings inward, and with the longer run its pin-side corner went into the hub land from
`maintHold` f ≈ 0.67, **0.056 deep at the stop**. intraUnit's MF tier caught it (`maintSpring` ⇄ the great wheel's
body).

TODO 291 fixed the incursion where it binds. The tip is now the shortest that keeps that corner, POSED AT THE STOP, one
margin off the land, bisected on the blade's own posed law (`dyAt` under `loadAt(run)`). The stub beyond the contact
carries no load, so no spring quantity moves. But the cut had wanted the face to run one pin radius (0.273) past the
contact, and under the hub that is not available at this run. The trimmed face runs **0.069** past the nearest contact
station (3.024; the stations slide to 3.212 by the stop, the tip ends at 2.955). The contact is on the face at every
pose, and the build guards that, but the pin bears near the face's end. `__clock.maintHoldLaw.tipOverhang` publishes
both numbers. Every gate is green; this is P1 debt, a margin spent on a P2 fit.

**Fix path.** Choose the ring's tooth count against the POSED tip, not only the run. "The fewest teeth whose pitch fits
the run" should read "the fewest whose run leaves the blade a pin radius of face past the contact with its tip clear of
the hub land at the stop". A count of 26 or more shortens the run, and with it the relaxation. That needs the posed-shape
law (the Maxwell kernel tabulated in the `MAINT_BLADE` block) moved into `MAINT_SPRING`'s solve, so the count search can
read it. Or re-site the pin outward (a larger `rP`), which carries the contact away from the hub land; the arm `rE`,
the face load and the blade's section all re-derive.

**Acceptance.** `tipOverhang.u ≥ tipOverhang.wanted_u` (a pin radius of face past the contact at every pose), the tip
one `CLEAR_MARGIN` off the hub land at every `maintHold` frame, `intraUnit` clean, and `equalisation`'s maintaining-hold
rows still under yield.
