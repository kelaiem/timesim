## 297. On the cut-to-shape form kinds the dial's feet stand at the round rim, off the dial and plates they join, and they set the case — DONE

Filed 2026-11-08, from BACKLOG §339 landing 2b (the chain pulley and one barrel). That landing re-ran the build's own
case fit (layout.js `fitFittedCase` / `fitCaseShape`, about the hands) on the per-unit hulls the build publishes
(`__clock.regions`), and found that the mainspring drum sets no form case: removing it moves no case at all. The fit's
regions record names what does, the 'Dial' unit on +x, −y and +y. Inside that unit it is the three dial feet.

**What is wrong.** The feet (main.js, `DIAL_FEET`, §125 step 5) are sited by the round rule: one `CLEAR_MARGIN` inside
the tighter of the dial's and the plate's RADII, `footR = min(dialRadius, plateR) − DIAL_FOOT_R − CLEAR_MARGIN`, at 10°,
108° and 250° about the hands. On the round build that puts each foot under the face it carries and on the plate it lands
on. On the cut-to-shape form kinds (`?case=rect`, `cushion`, `tonneau`, `fitted`) the dial, the base plate and the
three-quarter plate are cut to the form outline (§308) and no longer have that radius, and the feet stay where the round
rim was. On `alarm=0&case=fitted` (plates cut to x −38.17…34.70, y −35.54…41.20):

| foot | at (u) | against the cut |
|---|---|---|
| 10° | (40.1, 7.1) | **5.4 past** the dial's and the plates' +x edge (34.70) |
| 108° | (−12.6, 38.8) | inside the bounds |
| 250° | (−13.9, −38.3) | **2.8 past** their −y edge (−35.54) |

So two of the three posts stand off the dial they are meant to carry and off the plate they are meant to land on: a part
that looks placed and joins nothing, the failure the feet's own comment was written against. And because each is counted
metal, the case is fitted round it. `§186` and `§307/§308` already warn on these builds that the counted metal stands
inside the fit by less than the rim margin, and these are the posts behind it.

**What it costs, measured** (the build's own fit re-run without the three foot meshes; control: every unit re-fitted
reproduces the area the build cut, to 1e-5):

| build | as cut | the feet out | the feet and §339's drum group out |
|---|---|---|---|
| `alarm=0&case=fitted` | 1040.2 mm² (34.94 × 33.45 mm) | **944.5** (34.31 × 30.80) | 861.5 (33.97 × 27.66) |
| `case=fitted` | 1060.9 (34.66 × 33.45) | **1011.9** (34.68 × 31.90) | 1011.9 |
| `alarm=0&case=rect` | 1162.1 (34.65 × 33.63) | **1050.8** (33.89 × 31.09) | 1031.2 (33.89 × 30.51) |

The feet hold out 5–10% of every form case measured. They also hide the drum: with them inside, the drum group binds
the alarm-free builds, and §339's pulley and barrel would be worth up to another 83 mm² on the fitted case.

**Fix path.** §325 landing 2 has already fixed this for the free-outline kind (`FREE_DIAL_FEET`, measured there for the
same reason: "feet there held three corners of the outline out at the round size"). It re-sites each foot late, once
the plate→dial gap is built. A seat must clear every mesh in that gap, stand on metal of the plate it lands on, and stand
far enough inside the outline that it never sets it. The three are the seats, one per 120° sector, whose triangle holds
the hands deepest. Extend that solve to every cut-to-shape kind, reading the outline the plates are cut to (`FORM`).
The circularity the free kind meets (the outline is fitted to counted metal that includes the feet) is broken the same
way: the feet stand inside the outline by its own rim margin plus their radius, so they hold no side.

**Acceptance.** On every cut-to-shape kind, each foot stands wholly under the dial and on the base plate, a
`CLEAR_MARGIN` inside both cuts. No foot is the fitted case's binding member (`regions.binding`). The re-fit's area is
quoted per kind against the table above. `?formrect=` re-generated where a form point carries one, and the round build
bit-exact.
