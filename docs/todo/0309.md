## 309. A free case with the alarm cannot come under a narrow strap's cap

Filed 2026-11-14, out of §343 landing 4. A free case (`?case=free`) has no gong seat: `gongSeatFor` refuses the
`free` kind, so the build carries §198's ARC gong on the round case's annulus, at `GONG_R` 47.90 since the r 11.5
balance. Landing 4 made the free fit HOLD that arc (its wire and its block pinned as a seated gong's are), because the
outline fitted without it had cut through it. A ring of that radius sets the case's short side at 39.67 mm, whatever
the strap, so on every strap under 19 mm the build is over the owner's case limit (`2 × strap + 2`, §343 landing 2):

| strap | short side | limit | outline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 mm | 39.67 mm | 30 mm | 961.90 mm² |
| 15 mm | 39.67 mm | 32 mm | 968.01 mm² |
| 16 mm | 39.67 mm | 34 mm | 973.60 mm² |
| 17 mm | 39.67 mm | 36 mm | 982.51 mm² |
| 18 mm | 39.67 mm | 38 mm | 991.12 mm² |

Each boots `§307: the form case is 39.67 mm across its short side — over the owner cap` and makeFormCase's band
half-width row. The default strap (19 mm, 40 mm limit) and 20 mm come under it. Before landing 4, straps 15–18 read
under their caps only because the outline cut through the gong; strap 14 was over its cap either way (38.93 mm).

**What it needs.** A gong that FOLLOWS the free outline instead of standing on a circle: a free kind for
`tools/gong-seats.mjs`, the seat solved in the free outline's own band (the four box kinds' method, §310), its wire
pinned in the fit as the box kinds' seats are. Then the free case's width is set by the movement and the seat rather
than by the round annulus, and the narrow straps can be re-measured against their caps. Until then the warning is the
honest answer: those builds are over the limit, and they say so.

Done when a free build on a 14–18 mm strap carries a seated gong and comes under its strap's cap, or the cap's
derivation says why it cannot.
