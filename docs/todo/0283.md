**What is wrong.** §331 bores the band of a CONVEX form case (rect, cushion, tonneau) for the crown, alarm-crown and pusher tubes, by the round case's BORED-sector rule (`boredBand` in `src/form-case.js`). The free outline is a dense polyline whose triangles are short, so it may already read as contact, or it may sit where no swept point builds it. - the strip's crossings on a CONCAVE wall, since a strip may cross a wall more than twice, and each crossing pair is its own window;
- that the radial-face rule still closes the body.

Hold it with the same exact crossing count and `undeclaredClearance` on a free build. The free rows' fingerprints and fitted areas then move, so `tools/free-outline.mjs` regenerates `src/free-outlines.js`.

**Closed (2026-11-04).** The free band takes `boredBand` as it is: `formCaseLoops` already projects every free ring onto the
outer loop's vertices, so the two loops are index-aligned, and `boredBand` splits each wall at EVERY crossing of a strip's
edge and judges each cell by its own midpoint, so a concave wall crossed four times takes two windows with no special case,
and the radial faces stand wherever neighbouring cells' metal differs, convex or not. `src/form-case.js` builds every form
band that way now; the `prism` path is gone from the band.
