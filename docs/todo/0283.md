## 283. The free form case's band is not bored for its tubes: its sleeves stand in solid band metal

Filed 2026-11-03, alongside §331.

**What is wrong.** §331 bores the band of a CONVEX form case (rect, cushion, tonneau) for the crown, alarm-crown and pusher tubes, by the round case's BORED-sector rule (`boredBand` in `src/form-case.js`). The FREE outline (`?case=free`, the §325 arrangements and the lands preview) still builds its band as one earcut `prism` between its outer loop and its bore. That is the CSG debt the file's header used to state for every form case: each tube's sleeve, and the stem through it, stand in solid band metal.

**What is not known.** Whether the battery sees it. On `case=rect` the same debt read clean only because the band's sliver triangles blinded `meshClearance` (TODO 282). The free outline is a dense polyline whose triangles are short, so it may already read as contact, or it may sit where no swept point builds it. Measure first: the exact edge–triangle crossing count, `windStem` against `caseMiddle`, on the lands preview (`case=free&alarm=0&barrelstep=-140&rsvaz=0&rsvidl=2&frame=lands`) and on each validated free row.

**Fix path.** `boredBand` needs its two loops index-aligned. For the free outline they are by construction: `formCaseLoops` projects each ring onto the outer loop's vertices, so the bore keeps the outer's count and order. So the free band can take `boredBand` directly. Check two things first:
- the strip's crossings on a CONCAVE wall, since a strip may cross a wall more than twice, and each crossing pair is its own window;
- that the radial-face rule still closes the body.

Hold it with the same exact crossing count and `undeclaredClearance` on a free build. The free rows' fingerprints and fitted areas then move, so `tools/free-outline.mjs` regenerates `src/free-outlines.js`.
