Filed from the owner's report (2026-11-06): "four spiders floating" by the
keyless works — the bevel blanks at the setting cap and the setting rise,
standing in the air with nothing holding the shafts they are keyed to.

apex to the next, and every blank is keyed to a rod — but no rod runs in

cones, and the blank it belongs to is free to leave it in every direction a
bearing would forbid. `support` cannot see this because it is declared per
