## 270. Form builds: the governor window is cut to the round plate's rim, which a form plate does not have — DONE

**What is wrong.** The three-quarter plate's windows (`TQ_WINDOW_INTENTS`, §61/§115) are solved
by `solveTqWindows` against `tqOpeningClearance`. One of its terms is the round plate's rim,
`plateR − |p|`, held to `TQ_LAND_MIN`. Under `?case=` (§307/§308) the plate is cut to the form's
outline, which reaches past that circle, but the solve still reads the circle. So the window is
cut to a rim the form plate does not have.

The governor window shows it: the alarm's poising ring and fork, centred 34.24 from the movement's
axis (azimuth 62.2°), framed to a 6.483 disc. The window is the same, bearing for bearing, on the
round build and on every form build:
- 74 of its 360 bearings fall short of the disc, at 26°–99°, the side facing outward;
- every one of those 74 is bound by the round rim: its reach equals the solution of
  `plateR − |c + r·u| = TQ_LAND_MIN` to 1e-3;
- nothing else binds anywhere;
- the shortest reach is 5.377 = 41.516 − 34.239 − 1.900.

On the form builds, along those same bearings, the form plate holds `TQ_LAND_MIN` to the outline
over the whole disc:

| Case (`?formrect=` as `src/gong-seats.js` fits it) | Bearings that reach the full 6.483 | Outline inset at the disc's far edge |
|---|---|---|
| rect | 74 of 74 | 8.56 |
| cushion | 74 of 74 | 9.31 |
| tonneau | 74 of 74 | 10.10 |

So 5.89 u² of the governor's frame (4.5% of its disc, the outermost arc of the poising ring)
stays covered by plate that exists only because of a circle the case no longer has.

**The same coupling, three ways.**
- **The window is cut to the rim**, as above. Every window reads `tqOpeningClearance`, so the fusee
  window and the late arrest window carry the same rim term. Only the governor's is measured to
  bind today.
- **A window too near the outline is dropped whole.** `formFilter` drops a polygon if ANY vertex
  stands within the bevel land of the outline (all or nothing), rather than solving the window
  against the outline.
- **The window is solved before TODO 266's strike opening exists**, so the opening has to yield to
  it. TODO 266's land rule refused spiral stations at 1.571 of plate to "window 'governor'". §62's
  rule runs the other way ("where a wanted window and a required web cannot coexist, the WINDOW is
  what gives"), and the strike opening is a mechanism's clearance, not a view.

**Why no gate saw it.** Form builds are UNVERIFIED (TODO 158). On the round build the rim term is
right, and `checkPlateWindows` measures the cut windows only against openings and keeps.

**Fix path.**
- **Solve the windows against the plate that is cut.** Under FORM, `tqOpeningClearance`'s rim term
  becomes the outline's inset (`FORM.inset(x, y)`) held to the land `formFilter` already uses (the
  bevel plus `STOCK_MIN_U`). `formFilter` then has nothing to drop.
- **Let the window give to the strike opening.** Under FORM, solve the opening's swept hull (TODO
  266) as a keep the windows shrink around, or re-solve the windows at the re-cut with the opening
  in `tqPolyHoles`. That is the shrink-only re-cut §115 already allows.
- **Keep the round build bit for bit.** Its rim term stays; fingerprint 955951544.
- **Re-check the newly opened arc** against keeps the round rim was hiding. On the round build the
  rim bound first everywhere, so no other bound was ever read at those bearings.

These are P4/P3 changes (a view of the mechanism and the plate it is cut in), and no mechanism
dimension moves.

**Done when.**
- On each fitted form case the governor window reaches its full 6.483 at every bearing where the
  plate allows it, and checks `TQ_LAND_MIN` against the outline.
- `node tools/gong-seats.mjs --write` no longer refuses a station on the governor window's land.
  The window gives way instead, re-solved and still open all round, or §115's all-or-nothing rule
  refuses it and says so.
- The round build's fingerprint is unchanged.

**Done.** Under FORM only (the round build takes neither branch):
- **`tqOpeningClearance`'s rim term is the outline's inset**, held to `TQ_LAND_MIN`. That is more than
  `formFilter`'s 0.65 of bevel and stock, so `formFilter` has no window to drop.
- **The strike group's opening (TODO 266) is cut before the re-cut re-solves the windows.** It is an
  opening owed a land (`tqStrikeOpeningDist`), so the window gives way to it, as §62's rule says. The
  opening's own lands are read after, against the windows as re-solved.

The fusee and arrest windows read the same as before on every build, and the re-cut's shrink-only
check holds: nothing grew. A full `node tools/gong-seats.mjs --write` refuses no station on a
window's land; before this, a tonneau spiral was refused at 1.237 to the governor's.

**And two faults in the seat generator, found running it** (`tools/gong-seats.mjs`; `probe-310`'s B2
carries the first):
- **A seat may not raise a warning the movement as first fitted lacks.** A seat was admitted when its
  seated build added no warning the arc build at the SAME fit lacked. A warning the seat causes by
  widening the fit stands in both, so it was never counted. That is how both of §310 landing 3's spiral
  seats shipped over §307's 40 mm owner cap: the rect at 40.09 mm across, the tonneau at 41.25 mm. Both
  now compare against the first fit too, by kind (digits folded, because a measured warning's numbers
  drift with the fit). Both spiral seats are withdrawn, and TODO 269 says what that leaves.
- **A clean seat that fits inside the current cut is preferred**, and one that widens the cut is only
  the round's fallback. Taking the first clean seat sent the tonneau to a 53.2 cut it then could not
  hold.

An end-struck spiral's station is stored to 1e-12. At 1e-6 its pivot stood up to ~5e-6 off the lever's
circle, against the build's 1e-6 arm check.
