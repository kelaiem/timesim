## 294. The geared stop-work builds metal at all thirteen reserves and is recorded at two: the other ten have a pair, a branch arm and a cock stall that nothing reads

Filed 2026-11-08, out of §337 stage 3 (the record gate, #923). §337's entry has carried this as a sentence since landing
2b — "**Still owed by stage 3:** the other ten geared reserves and 39 h's 0.78 of bulge" — with no item number, so it was
on no list anyone reads to pick up work.

**What is wrong.** `GEARED_BUILT` is `SPEC.arrest === 'geared'` (`src/layout.js:517`), with none of the sited-hours gate
`STOPWORK_SITED_HOURS` puts on §329's direct stop-work. The geared key therefore cuts metal at every one of the thirteen
reserves, not at the two §337 sited. Measured on 6e008282, booting `?arrest=geared` at three reserves this record does
not cover:

| reserve | the pair built | ratio | branch arm root (u) | cock bar, of yield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 39 h | 20 : 26 | 0.7692 | 2.788 | 0.779 |
| 42 h | 20 : 28 | 0.7143 | 2.814 | 0.809 |
| 48 h | 15 : 24 | 0.6250 | 2.581 | 0.757 |

Each reads `built: true` and `geared: true`, each solves its pair, each sizes its branch arm, and each stands under yield
on the statics reading with `stallWaiver.reading` `statics` — the same waiver the two built reserves take. 39 h's pair is
the 20 : 26 landing 2a named as its only ratio.

So the debt is **not** that the other ten have no metal. It is that the metal they have has no reader. `docs/built/0337.md`
quotes the arrangement, the bulge with the arm and the whole stall table at 24 and 36 h only; `tools/probe-337-record.mjs`
holds exactly those two, because it holds the table it parses; the two battery spec points (`geared-24`, `geared-36`) boot
those two. At the other ten, every figure in the table above is computed at boot and then dropped on the floor. Landing 2a
says so in its own words: "Only 24 and 36 h are measured; the other ten owe the same reading."

**Part of this IS read, at thirteen.** `tools/probe-337-geared-geneva.mjs` is ACCEPTANCE, runs twice in
`.github/workflows/battery.yml`, and its `HOURS` is all thirteen reserves: it holds each reserve's pair against the star's
own stepped travel, its cuttability, the Geneva's floors, the pin and stud under `SPRING_SIGMA_Y_PA` at that reserve's own
stall torque, and that the orbit is the smallest the line may take. So the PAIR and the Geneva's sizing already have a
reader everywhere. The two members with none are the ones that need the FOLD — the branch arm's root width and the cock
stall — and the heading above should be read as naming those. It is not re-worded because it cannot be:
`tools/check-item-numbers.mjs` holds a TODO's title as its IDENTITY (`titleIsIdentity`), so a changed title under a
standing number is a collision by design.

The fold's plan search and the bulge search DID cover all thirteen (landing 2a: every reserve has an arrangement, none over
0.8 u of bulge). What is measured at two is the quantity the build actually needs — the bulge **with the branch arm counted**,
and the arm's root width, which feeds back into the cock bar's own sizing because the bar must carry the studs' reactions.
That coupling is explicitly "the build's, not this probe's", and it has been closed at two reserves.

**39 h is the outlier the table already names.** 0.782 u of bulge against 0.250 at the next worst (27 h); a fold site of
−0.782 before the bulge; and its only ratio putting the finger 4.6 out. It is the one reserve where the answer may be a
different line rather than more relief.

**Why this is worth a number.** It is the same failure class stage 3 just closed for the two built reserves. Eleven figures
in §337's measured table had gone stale under TODO 291's torque change and nothing said so until the record gate was
written. The other ten reserves are in precisely the state those two were in before it: figures with no reader, free to
move under the next torque change without a word. Every stall row above reads off the going torque TODO 291 already moved
once.

**Fix path.**
1. **Measure the ten.** `probe-337-bulge.mjs` already searches all thirteen and already counts the arm; record its
   bulge-with-arm and arm root width per reserve, extending landing 2a's two-row table to thirteen.
2. **Re-size the cock bar against the worst of them.** The bar's root already stands at the yield (172 of 172 MPa) at
   R = 4.6 N before the studs' reactions are added, and the WIDEST arm of the twelve is what it must carry. All twelve are
   now read (`tools/probe-337-geared-invariant.mjs`, on 144c746b, 2026-11-08): the widest arm the BUILD publishes is
   **27 h at 3.409 u**, half a unit wider than either recorded reserve (24 h 2.891, 36 h 1.993), and the bar stands at
   0.817 of yield there — so the section already carries a governing load it was never sized against, which is a finding
   and not a licence. That reading also exposed a TWO-READER disagreement to settle first: landing 2a's table records the
   arm as 3.304 at 24 h and 2.75 at 36 h, where the geared boot publishes 2.891 and 1.993 for the same member under the
   same law (b = sqrt(6M/(T·sigma_y))). Landing 2a's figures are `probe-337-bulge.mjs`'s own arithmetic off a DEFAULT
   boot and predate the lock fix that moved 24 h's pair from 20 : 16 to 25 : 20, and `probe-337-record.mjs` does not parse
   that table, so nothing holds it either way. Which reader is right decides what "the widest" even is.
3. **Settle 39 h** — more relief, a different (m, n), or a recorded refusal at that one reserve.
4. **Then gate what gets recorded.** `probe-337-record.mjs` derives its reserves from the table it parses, so a thirteen-row
   table is held at thirteen reserves with no change to the probe — but at roughly 25 s a boot that is a battery-cap
   question before it is a code question (the probes job is capped at 25 min on nine samples).
5. **Or refuse what is not sited.** The alternative to measuring the ten is to give `GEARED_BUILT` the sited-hours gate
   §329 has, so the key refuses an unmeasured reserve instead of quietly cutting metal for it. That is a smaller landing
   and it makes the record true by construction, at the cost of the owner's "a Geneva at every reserve".
6. **Or gate the INVARIANT and record no new rows.** Both unread members are already SOLVED at runtime, not sited: the
   branch arm's width comes straight out of its bending moment at yield (`src/main.js`, the fold's `const armW =
   Math.sqrt(...)`, `armW = sqrt(6(Ff·D + Fs·dS) / (t·sigma_y))` — 51990 on 144c746b), and the cock stall is swept
   over both stops and the whole DERIVED pressure-angle range, the worst section taken, and compared to the bar's,
   leg's, post's and pin-shear yields (`src/main.js`, the `over(cockStall.statics)` warn — 52338 on 144c746b). What
   is missing there is not the reading but an instrument that FAILS on it — the comparison `console.warn`s, and a
   geared boot already carries 6 to 38 warnings of that reserve's own standing debt, so silence cannot be read as a
   pass. **This is built**: `tools/probe-337-geared-invariant.mjs` boots all thirteen under the key and holds seven
   rules on the PUBLISHED `WIND_ARREST.stopwork` — the set is whole (B); a direct line stands only where
   `STOPWORK_SITED_HOURS` sites one (D); the pair is cuttable and the published ratio is its own quotient (P); the
   arm is solved and its studs loaded (A); every member of the stall is at or under its yield on statics, read
   twice and the two readers made to agree (S); the root moment is inside the moment the bar was cut for (M); and
   the gating reading is the same everywhere (R). Each rule is re-run against a deliberately broken copy of every
   reserve's own reading and must refuse it. It gates every reserve with no thirteen-row table, and does it on the
   METAL rather than on a model. Cost, measured 2026-11-08: the thirteen boots wall 414 s in all (25-37 s each,

   runner's 2 lanes, ~300 s). The run that executed it — 42521704332, the job 1154 s, its probe step 1126 s — said
