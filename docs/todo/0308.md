## 308. §186's case-sector tripwire judges the setting lever unposed

Filed 2026-11-14, out of a case-volume study of the round alarm-free build. The §186 tripwire (`main.js` ~53900, "(b)
The tripwire: at the build pose") walks every movement mesh's edges for metal in the case annulus and warns
`§186: the analytic case sectors' annulus is OCCUPIED at the build pose`. It runs during the build, before the first
`tick(0)` (`main.js:66533`). The setting lever's angle is written only by `tick()`
(`settingLeverGroup.rotation.z = settingLeverAngleAt(crownPullT)`, `main.js:63791`), so when the tripwire reads it the
group still has the `rotation.z = 0` a new `THREE.Group` is born with. That is a pose the movement never takes.

**What it does.** Measured off the booted metal (the lever's own meshes, reach from the centre arbor, movement units):

| lever pose | default `alarm=0` | `alarm=0&stemaz=135&d4=16&escstep=-50&balstep=70&drumaz=68` |
|---|---|---|
| pushed in (`crownPullT` 0) | 40.868 (rotation −0.594) | 35.406 (rotation −3.682) |
| pulled (`crownPullT` 1) | 38.463 (rotation −0.104) | 33.140 (rotation −4.172) |
| **unposed, rotation 0** | 38.951 (beak) | **40.658** (beak) |

On the default arrangement the unposed lever happens to sit inside the bore (`CASE_R_IN` 44.155), so the tripwire is
silent and right for the wrong reason. With the stem turned (`?stemaz=135`) the lever rests about 211° from its unposed
angle and the unposed beak reaches 40.658. With `CASE_CLEAR` at 0.9 mm (`CASE_R_IN` 40.534) the tripwire fires on
`settingLeverBody, settingLeverBeak, settingLeverBeakPin`; at 1.0 mm (`CASE_R_IN` 40.798) it does not. The lever's real
metal at both ends of the pull stands 5.1 u inside that bore. So the warning names a fault that is not there, and it
would equally pass a real one on any member whose unposed angle happens to sit inboard.

The setting lever is the instance measured. Any member whose transform only `tick()` writes (the yoke, the jumper, the
reset and hack links) is read unposed by the same walk.

**Fix path.** CLAUDE.md's "boot has no pose" rule applies: a claim about a posed movement is an instrument's job, not a
boot assert's, and `tick(0)` stands the movement at the saved session's pose, so moving the walk after it does not fix
it either.
1. Restrict the boot tripwire to metal whose transform is fixed at build time: skip any mesh under a group a pose law
   writes (start with the keyless works' movers), so it holds the pose-invariant claim it can actually make.
2. Hold the movers in the annulus where the pose net already exists: `probe-case-relief` (rides `battery.yml`) or the
   battery's own sweeps, at both ends of the crown pull.

**Acceptance.** On `alarm=0&stemaz=135&d4=16&escstep=-50&balstep=70&drumaz=68` with `CASE_CLEAR` at 0.9 mm the tripwire
is silent. Control: move the setting lever's pivot outward until its posed beak enters the annulus, and the instrument
that now owns the movers fails. The default build's fingerprint is unchanged (the tripwire only warns).
