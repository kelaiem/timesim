## 298. The battery cannot judge a build without the alarm: its tables name the alarm's units, and the alarm-free build has never been swept

Filed 2026-11-09, out of BACKLOG §339 2b′. The owner's question there was whether a different going-train
arrangement could shrink the alarm-free fitted case (`?alarm=0&case=fitted`). That needs the battery's verdict on an
alarm-free build, and it was the first time anyone asked for one.

**What is wrong.** §325 landing 3 made the alarm a MODULE: `?alarm=0` builds the movement without it, and `main.js`
branches on `ALARM_BUILT` at 103 sites to leave the module's metal uncut. `src/inspect.js` has no notion of that at all.
Its tables (`MECH_GRAPH`, the hand-off rows, `PLATE_SEATS`, `INTRA_UNIT_CONTACTS` and its floors, `LUBRICATION`, the
penetration budgets, the clearance tables, the expected-contact floors, the line specs) are written for the build WITH
the alarm. Run against an alarm-free build, they fail on every row that names an alarm unit, and three checks throw.

Measured with the native battery (`node --import ./tools/native/loader.mjs tools/battery-native.mjs --query
'alarm=0&case=fitted&barrelstep=-20&rsvaz=0&alarmmod=50&rsvidl=2' --shards 4`, the alarm-free fitted case at the
default arrangement, on c19459f8, this branch: main at 14a1f8c1 plus §339's report probes), 13 gates fail and 3 throw.

**Real findings on the alarm-free metal** (what a module-aware battery must leave standing):

- **The setting lever stands in the case middle**: `inspection` FORBIDDEN `Case ⇄ Setting lever`, `undeclaredClearance`
  0 at `beat f=0`, `sweptOverlap` CONFIRMED (0.793 overlap), and two `plateSeats` rows (`hackRodPin` and a cylinder of
  the setting lever, contact). One defect seen by four checks.
- `plateSeats`' `Case / caseMiddle` seat is STALE on this build (never within the margin of the plate).
- `outlines`: the three-quarter plate's outline crosses itself once (rings 144/146 at (7.81, −35.48)).
- `restoring`: the maintaining detent and the minute jumper read restored by nothing (`fanout 0`). Whether that is the
  metal or an alarm-named declaration is not yet read.

**Why it matters.** Every alarm-free build (round, the four form kinds, `case=free`) is unvalidated: none is in
`src/validated-configs.js`, and the one alarm-free spec point (`stopwork-140`, `case=free` at its own arrangement) is
swept by the point tier's three sweeps only. So a part through another part on an alarm-free build ships under a green
board, and the setting lever above is one. It also means no arrangement can be adopted for the alarm-free builds:
§339 2b′ measured escape −30° / balance 40° at 902.7 mm² against today's 944.5, with no finding beyond the
reference's, and could not land it, because "no finding beyond the reference's" is not a verdict CI can give.

**Fix path.**
1. **Module awareness.** One declaration of which `MECH_GRAPH` units the alarm module owns, and the build publishing
   which modules it built. Every table row naming an absent module's unit then stands down, is counted, and is
   reported by name. Three controls:
   - On the default build nothing stands down.
   - On an alarm-free build every declared module unit is absent from the scene. A declared unit present while its
     module is absent fails, and so does an absent unit that is not declared.
   - A row standing down names only absent units of absent modules. A row naming a missing unit of a BUILT module
     still fails exactly as now.
2. **The real findings**: the setting lever out of the case middle, the plate outline's crossing, the two `restoring`
   rows read and fixed or waived against this item.
3. **An alarm-free spec point** in `tools/ci-battery.mjs`' `SPEC_POINTS`, swept, so CI judges the alarm-free metal on
   every PR. That point is where an alarm-free arrangement (§339 2b′'s escape −30° / balance 40°) can then be judged.

**Landing 1, 2026-11-09 — the battery stands a row down on the build's own word** (9ce94097). The build publishes
`clock.modules` (`{ alarm: ALARM_BUILT }`) and `clock.omitted`. `clock.omitted` lists each mesh its solve left uncut,
pushed at the site that decides it and only with the reason the build gave: TODO 289's unsited rise and cap bearings
(warned at boot), and the drop's foot bush, which the siting refused with a reason and replaced with a bearing from
above. `inspect.js` declares `MODULE_UNITS.alarm` (30 units). `buildOmissions` / `leftOut` are the one reading every
check uses.
- **Every site that resolved a table row against the scene now stands it down** when, and only when, one of its names
  is left out: support, the graph (`missingFromScene` and its anchors), penetration, every hand-off table,
  `plateSeats`, `intraUnit` (selectors and floors), `lubrication`, `clearances`, `expectedContacts` and `lineSpec`
  (each reference declares `module: 'alarm'`).
- **The rows keep their place.** `clearances` and `expectedContacts` keep one row per budget, `standDown` set, so the
  §127/§152 merges still align them by index, and the extrema merge carries the list through.
- **A new `modules` gate holds the word true of the scene both ways.** Its five controls feed the judge broken copies of
  this build's own word: every module flipped, a module with no word, an omission over a present mesh, an omission with
  no reason, and a name the build never gave.
- **Measured, native battery on this container:**
  - The default build reads 36/36. Its report is identical to main's: 35 checks, 186,640 leaves, 0 differences.
    `standDown` is carried only when non-empty, which is why.
  - The alarm-free fitted case runs every check, none throws, and 29/36 pass. Its `modules` gate reads 30 units absent,
    8 meshes omitted, controls PASS.

**What the seven failures that remain are**: every one a real finding, now visible because nothing hides it.

- **The form case's own rows**, failing on the fitted case WITH the alarm too, so they belong to the case shape and not

**Landing 2, 2026-11-09 — the findings, read to their causes** (90d26ce7). Each of the seven had one cause, and four of
the seven were one defect:
- **The alarm-free box form cases were never fitted.** `FORM_SEAT_FIT` answers only where the alarm is built (the gong
  seat's fixed point carries the fit), so every `?alarm=0` box kind was cut to the solvers' `formPlate`, the first boot's
  guess. Its counted metal stood 1.611 u outside the outline (§307/§308's own boot warning), the case middle ran through
  the setting lever (`inspection`, `undeclaredClearance`, `sweptOverlap`, `plateSeats`), and the three-quarter plate's
  outline crossed itself.
  - **The fix.** A box kind's fit is one boot from its own fixed point: booted at the fit it measures, it measures the
    same fit, on all four kinds. `tools/form-fits.mjs` iterates to that and writes `src/form-fits.js` (GENERATED; `--check`
    boots each kind bare and holds that it was cut from the table and that the row is still its own fixed point).
    `main.js` cuts an alarm-free box case to its row.
  - **Measured at its own fit:** the lever clears the case by 5.17, as on the alarm build. The dropped plate openings,
    §186's occupied sectors and the crossing go with it.
- **`restoring`'s two rows: the declarations were inside `buildAlarm()`.** §325 landing 3 carried the minute jumper's and
  the maintaining detent's springs in with the alarm's, so an alarm-free build declared nothing that returns either part.
  They are `declareGoingRestoring()` now, called where they stood on the alarm build (its declaration order, and the
  default's report, unchanged) and after the skipped call on the build without.
- **The form case's two gasket rows** describe the round case's I-ring (TODO 122). The form case presses its glass under
  the lip with none, by design, so the build records that as an omission.
- **`plateSeats`' `caseClampScrew` and `caseMiddle` are real, and not this item's:** a form case holds its movement by
  nothing the battery can see, with or without the alarm. Filed as **TODO 300**.
- **Measured (native battery):** the default build 36/36, its report unmoved apart from main's own TODO 296 row. The
  alarm-free fitted case 35/36, failing only TODO 300's two rows.

**Open, this item's: the seconds well past the form track's inner rail.** At its own fit the fitted case's dial track
comes inside the round rail the seconds well was solved against, and the well stands 1.096 u inside the track's inner
rail against `CLEAR_MARGIN` (§311's boot warning; it read −5.824 at the guessed cut). The alarm build's larger case does
not reach it. The fix is the well's solve read on the form rail it prints, which §311's comment already names.

**Landing 3 — an alarm-free spec point.** `alarm-free-fitted` (`alarm=0&case=fitted`) in `tools/ci-battery.mjs`'
`SPEC_POINTS`, swept, booting exactly four filed rows: §186's (TODO 300), TODO 289's two, and §311's well (above). So
CI's point tier sweeps the alarm-free metal on every run, and a part through another on it no longer ships green.

**Acceptance.** `battery-native.mjs --query` on the alarm-free fitted case runs every check with none throwing, every
failing row is a real finding (not an absent unit), the three controls above hold, and an alarm-free point sweeps in CI.
