**Why, in four groups** (world boxes of the units, arrangement against default, read off a boot of each):

1. **The swung barrel sits on the third wheel.** `barrelstep=0` turns the fusee from its designed 145° to 180°
   (its unit's box moves from x −35.1…8.6, y −12.5…31.1 to x −32.2…−0.2, y ±16.0: centre (−16.2, 0), r 16),
   and the THIRD WHEEL does not move at all (x −21.3…6.5, y −24.3…3.5 in both builds: centre ≈ (−7.4, −10.4),
   r ≈ 13.9). The centres stand 13.6 apart against radii summing to ~30, and the two share z (the cone and the
   wheel's extrude cross; the chain's run from the drum to the cone crosses the wheel's rim on the way). Landing 5
   gave the barrel's frame to five SATELLITES (the stem, the reserve, the alarm module, the minute wheel, the
   fusee windows) and left the going train's own stations where `solveLayout` puts them for the designed step —
   the third wheel is a train station, not a satellite, and nothing turned it. The probe's warning-set gate could
   not see it: no boot assert measures the fusee against the third wheel.
2. **The alarm link's crank B stands where the swing brought the barrel.** The link's measured site at the 80°
   turn was cleared against the MODULE's own boxes (landing 5's module-frame obstacles) and against nothing the
   barrel's swing moved under it: the alarm winding arrest's sub-idler pinion (the arrest's unit now at
   x −16.9…−3.9, y 16.5…28.6, from x 14.6…30.9, y 2.1…13.2), the chain's run and the drum's hook tab (the drum at
   x −34.6…−13.2, y 6.3…27.7, from x −20.5…0.9, y 16.9…39.6) all meet crank B.
3. **The turned stem under the rect's case middle.** With the crown pulled (f ≥ 0.94, and every hand-set and

   not cut for the pulled stem at the turned azimuth — and the hack rod, re-routed with the stem, meets the fork
   cock's turned body at the same poses.
4. **The gong and the hammer.** The gong's arc runs on the round case's path inside the form wall at every pose

**What a fix must hold.** The arrangement's point sweep CLEAN — the battery measures it on every push and names
the rows — and only then `node tools/validated-configs.mjs --write --points FILE` from a whole run's points
file, which is what retires the UNVERIFIED mark on a form build for the first time. Nothing is waived here: a
finding keeps a point out of the set and fails no gate, and that is the honest state until the metal moves.

**Fix paths, in the record's priority order** (P3 — position space, never a lever arm or a budget):

- Group 1 is the layout's. Either the going train's stations past the centre take the barrel's turn as the
  satellites do (the third wheel's bearing follows `FUSEE_TURN_DEG`, so the train turns with its barrel and the
  escapement and balance with it — the five satellites are the template), or the third wheel gets a step of its
  own (`escapestep` and `balstep` exist; a `thirdstep` does not), or the arrangement takes a barrel step between
  the design's −35 and 0 at which the cone clears the wheel — the landing-5 grid measured the width at −20 and
  −10 too, and the swing's whole point was the width, so the smallest turn that clears the alarm module is the one

- Group 2 is the link's measured-site solve: the obstacle set it clears has to carry the arrest's idler, the
  chain's swept run (§36's registry has it) and the drum's hook tab wherever the barrel's frame puts them, not
  only the module's own boxes; a site that clears them all at 80° may not exist, which is a layout finding for
  group 1's table rather than a reason to shorten crank B.
- Group 3 is the form case's: cut the stem opening for the pulled stem at the turned azimuth (the round case's
  tube standoff is derived from the plate's rim; the rect's wall is not that rim), and site the hack rod's run
  off the fork cock as §232's lay shaft was sited, by measurement.
- Group 4: the gong's form path is §308's open item already; the collet's hairline is 0.0013 and belongs with
