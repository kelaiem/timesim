Every form case builds an ARRANGED movement by default (§310 landing 4, then §313): the going barrel swung to

changes against the round default and unioned by §152's rule — and it reads FINDING. This item is its list.

**How it got here.** The item was filed on §308 landing 5's arrangement (`?case=rect&barrelstep=0&rsvaz=36&
alarmmod=80&gong=arc`, its metal-centred fit), the first form build swept, which read 8 FORBIDDEN pairs and 9
undeclared ones. §310 landing 4 then cased the form cases about the hands and §313 moved their default to the
arrangement above, so the swept row moved with it (`arrangement-308` is now a declared boot only). Against the
80° build:
- **Kept**, identical to the pose: the fusee and the chain in the third wheel, the pulled stem in the case
  middle, the hack rod in the fork cock, the hammer collet's hairline. Crank B is still in the winding arrest,
  now on its leg-B pinion where it met the sub-idler at 80°.
- **Gone**: crank B in the chain's run and the drum's hook tab (the 60° module puts it elsewhere); the gong's
  arc in the case wall (the default gong is a seated bar, not the case path).
- **New**: the release sleeve's guide in the selector's tab at every pose, and the link's centre roller 0.0177
  off the same guide.
- Measured on the default rect at §310 landing 4's fit, before §313, the same eight rows held and one more
  hairline appeared (the pusher stem 0.1463 off a pillar at the press's f 0.39); §313's arrangement does not
  carry it.

**Why, in four groups.**

1. **The swung barrel sits on the third wheel.** `barrelStepDeg 0` turns the fusee from its designed 145° to
   180° (its unit's box from x −35.1…8.6, y −12.5…31.1 to x −32.2…−0.2, y ±16.0: centre (−16.2, 0), r 16), and
   the THIRD WHEEL does not move at all (x −21.3…6.5, y −24.3…3.5 in both builds: centre ≈ (−7.4, −10.4),
   r ≈ 13.9). The centres stand 13.6 apart against radii summing to ~30, and the two share z. §308 landing 5 gave
   the barrel's frame to five satellites (the stem, the reserve, the alarm module, the minute wheel, the fusee
   windows) and left the going train's own stations where `solveLayout` puts them for the designed step; §313
   kept that. No boot assert measures the fusee against the third wheel.
2. **The turned alarm module meets its neighbours.** Crank B's inner arm stands in the winding arrest's leg-B
   pinion, and the release sleeve's first guide in the selector's tab, at every pose; the link's centre roller
   passes the same guide at 0.0177. The link's measured site was cleared against the module's own boxes
   (§308 landing 5's module-frame obstacles), not against the arrest's or the sleeve's metal where the turn
   brought them. The sleeve and the selector are both alarm units, so the second row may be the alarm group
   disagreeing with itself (P2) rather than a fit (P3); read it before choosing which way to move.
3. **The pulled stem and the hack rod.** With the crown pulled (f ≥ 0.94, and every hand-set and minute-jumper
   pose) the wind stem meets the form case's `caseMiddle` — the stem opening is not cut for the pulled stem at the
   turned azimuth — and the hack rod, re-routed with the stem, meets the fork cock's body at the same poses.
4. **The hammer's collet** passes the three-quarter plate 0.0013 under the margin at the strike's f 0.8165.

**What a fix must hold.** The `form-rect` point sweep CLEAN — the battery measures it on every push and names the
rows — and only then `node tools/validated-configs.mjs --write --points FILE` from a whole run's points file,
which retires the UNVERIFIED mark on a form build for the first time. Nothing is waived: a finding keeps a point
out of the set and fails no gate, and that is the honest state until the metal moves.

**Fix paths** (P3 — position space, never a lever arm or a budget; group 2's sleeve row first read as P2):

- Group 1 is the layout's. Either the going train's stations past the centre take the barrel's turn as the
  satellites do (the third wheel's bearing follows `FUSEE_TURN_DEG`, so the train turns with its barrel and the
  escapement and balance with it — the five satellites are the template), or the third wheel gets a step of its
  own (`escapestep` and `balstep` exist; a `thirdstep` does not), or the arrangement takes a barrel step between
  the design's −35 and 0 at which the cone clears the wheel, re-measuring the width the swing was for.
- Group 2 is the link's measured-site solve and the module's turn: the obstacle set the site clears has to carry
  the arrest's pinions and the sleeve's guides wherever the turn puts them, not only the module's own boxes. A
  site that clears them all at 60° may not exist, which is a layout finding for §313's turn table rather than a
  reason to shorten crank B.
- Group 3 is the form case's: cut the stem opening for the pulled stem at the turned azimuth (the round case's
  tube standoff is derived from the plate's rim; the rect's wall is not that rim), and site the hack rod's run
  off the fork cock by measurement.
- Group 4: the collet's hairline is 0.0013 and belongs with whichever of the above moves the hammer's station,
  measured again after.
