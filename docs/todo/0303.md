## 303. A cushion case's flat lugs stand proud of its curved end wall

Filed 2026-11-13, out of TODO 300's boots. Every cushion build warns at boot, with or without the alarm (`case=cushion`,
`alarm=0&case=cushion`):

> makeFormCase: the cushion's ±y end wall falls 1.436 u from the lug plane at the lugs' outboard faces (27.708 u off the
> centreline) — over CLEAR_MARGIN 0.15: the flat lugs stand proud of a curved end

That reading is main's at f12ecaf8, 1.441 with the alarm. Since TODO 300 cut the case to the fitted bore, which draws the
outer wall in by 2.22 u, it reads 1.487 alarm-free and 1.492 with the alarm. No spec point builds a cushion, so the spec
tier has never seen the warning.

**What is wrong.** Each lug is ONE flat outline from `caseLugPlan`, welded to the plane through the end wall at the strap's
centreline (`form-case.js`, `rOut`, the outer shape's exit along ±y). The cushion's end is a superellipse (`|x'/a|⁴ +
|y'/b|⁴ = 1`), so across the strap's width the wall falls away from that plane. At the lugs' outboard faces, `lugCtrSpan /
2 + CASE_LUG_T / 2` off the centreline, it stands about 1.4–1.5 u short. So the outboard half of each lug is welded to
nothing: a flat lug proud of a curved end, 10× the margin. The rect and the tonneau end flat or nearly so at the lugs, and
the fitted tonneau's end is held near flat by its own fit. The cushion is the one kind whose end curves across the strap.

**Fix path** (the lug is STRUCTURAL; the case fits the strap, the strap does not move):
1. Weld each lug to the wall it meets. Cut the lug's inboard face on the end wall's own curve over its width, a fillet run
   along `outerShape.exit`, the same ray the check reads. The lug then stands on metal across its whole thickness.
2. If the joint's root section then falls under the lug's load floor (`caseLugPlan` sizes it by the strap load for the
   weakest alloy, TODO 262), carry the weld deeper, never thinner.
3. The boot check then holds the welded face's gap to the wall, not a plane's fall.

**Acceptance.** `case=cushion` and `alarm=0&case=cushion` boot without the warning, with no lug section under its floor
and the strap gap (`lugSpanInner`) unchanged.
