## §318 — The form cases' alarm pusher turns off the seated strike group (links yield)

The owner found it on the form cases: on §314's arrangement every form case seats the SPIRAL gong, and the seat
put the hammer's arm and head through the alarm pusher's blue reach bar, at rest. Two gaps let it ship. The seat
generator judged the hammer only at the wire's height, and the alarm pusher's metal runs above that band. And
nothing at boot reads the hammer against other units' metal. TODO 274 had recorded the pair as a finding.

**The rule it set.** CLAUDE.md's P3 now carries the owner's precedence: when two members contest the same space,
a LINK (a member that only transfers motion) yields to one that is STRUCTURAL or that ACTS. A link is re-routed
around the member it meets, never the other way round.

**Why the pusher is a link, and how it moves.** Since §261 2c the pusher's pin no longer bears on the column
wheel: it drives crank A, and the linkage carries the press to the driver's own track, which the wheel fixes. The
press line was still derived from the wheel (§170: radial, at the coupling's offset), so the reach bar lay across
the hammer, whose arm spans that line from post to head at every height from z 4 to 10. No re-route ALONG the line
exists, and lowering the bar still crosses the head. So the whole pusher turns about the movement's centre:
`ALARM_PUSH_ROT` turns `_pushU`, `_pushPerp` and `_pushBase` (stem, reach, riser, return spring and the case's
tube all follow), while the driver's coupling (`pinRest`) and the linkage's folded driver track (q0, q1) stay on
the wheel's line (`_pushU0`, `_pushBase0`). The turn is a per-build table, `ALARM_PUSH_TURNS`, keyed by the seat's
spec and mode; a build with no row keeps the wheel's line, the round build included. A turned pusher reads its own
linkage line, `ALARM_LINKAGE_LINES[<arrangement>|turn N]`.

**The pick: 48°.** `tools/probe-318-pusher-turn.mjs` boots trial turns and measures the pusher's metal against
every labelled mesh over the press and the strike. Its control is the unturned build reading the strike group
under the margin. On the rect, over −60…60° in 6° steps:

| turn | the pusher's metal |
|---|---|
| −60 … −24 | the hairspring, the stop lever, the balance or its cock |
| −18 | clear |
| −12 | the gong's wire |
| −6, 6 | the hammer |
| 12, 18 | clear |
| 24 … 42 | the winding arrest's leg-B pinion, a pillar, the striking wheel |
| 48, 54, 60 | clear |

`tools/probe-314-linkage-line.mjs --turn` (new flag) then re-searched the linkage at each clear turn. At 12 and
18, crank B's stud meets the winding arrest's leg-B pinion: 3,769 and 3,602 lines were judged and none clears, the
nearest a cell off. At −18 the rod crosses the hammer's arm. From 48 the search clears. 48 is the smallest turn
that carries a linkage, and its shortest line (96°, c 27.34) is 28.524 long against the unturned line's 52.51. The
cushion and tonneau read the same at 48: pusher clear, same line.

**The strike opening, held between samples.** With the pusher clear, the rect's sweep read one hairline: the
hammer's tail 0.1484 off the three-quarter plate at the strike's f 0.7156. The plate's strike opening (TODO 266)
was the hull of twelve sampled swing poses grown one margin, and between samples the metal bows past each chord by
the sagitta R(1 − cos(step/2)). That was 0.002 at the tail's ~8 u reach on a 0.045 rad step, the measured
shortfall. The opening now grows by that sagitta too, and its margin circle's 24-gon is circumscribed rather than
inscribed. The rect's sweep restricted to the alarm switch, the hammer and the case then reads 0 FORBIDDEN and 0
undeclared pairs under the margin.

**The seat generator** judges the hammer at its own heights: `gong-solve`'s `map.hammer` carries the post's
height (for the pivot) and the arm's with the head's (for the arm's line and the head's travel). Every seated
candidate is swept the battery's way (`inspection` and `undeclaredClearance` restricted to the hammer) and refused
for any pair its arc build at the same fit does not carry. The alarm switch is left out of both: its members are
links, they yield, and this section's turn plus the battery's form sweep hold the result.
