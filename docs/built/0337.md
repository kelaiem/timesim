## §337 — The geared Geneva stop-work: a finger wheel geared off the cone, so every reserve has an exact star

### Landing 1 (2026-11-06): the line

**Why.** §329's Geneva stop-work is the default arrest, but it stands only at 30 h. A star of n slots, m of them open, stops
its finger after exactly m + ½ + 1/n turns, and the fusee's cone winds reserveHours·7/120 turns. With the finger on the cone
those agree only at 30 h and 45 h, and 45 h finds no site, so every other reserve falls back to §47's stop iron. The owner
asked for a Geneva at every reserve. Driving the finger from the cone through a wheel pair makes its travel g·T, and a pair
chosen so g·T = m + ½ + 1/n stops it exactly, with no trim.

**The line** (`src/geared-geneva.js`, pure and movement-independent, reading `src/stopwork-arrest.js` rather than restating
it). Input to output:
1. the cone's wheel (z_cone), fast on the cone's arbor;
2. the finger's pinion (z_finger), on the finger's own arbor;
3. a crank on that arbor carrying the pin at r1;
4. the star of n slots, at a = r1/sin(π/n) from the finger's axis, its closed slots the two stops.

The pin cannot ride the pinion: a 6-leaf pinion at the module floor has a pitch radius near 0.6, inside which no pin and wall
fit. A crank and its pinion on one hub is the alarm Geneva's own arrangement (§129, TODO 212). Where g is exactly one the
line needs no gear at all: the finger is the cone, §329's direct stop-work.

Every number is derived:

| quantity | rule |
|---|---|
| the ratio | g = 60·(2mn + n + 2)/(7·h·n), exact as a reduced fraction (the reserve train's rule: no float reaches a tooth count) |
| the module | the §50 floor: a tooth π·module/2 thick at its pitch line is `STOCK_MIN_U`, so 0.202 u |
| the counts | the reduced pair scaled by the least whole k so that the pinion has at least `PINION_LEAVES_MIN` (6) leaves and the cone's wheel's root circle stands a §50 wall outside the cone's arbor (`TRAIN_STAFF_R`, 0.5) |
| the load | the stall at the cone is the fusee's level torque (the same at both stops); the finger holds it divided by g, so the pin carries F = stall/(g·r1) |
| the pin and stud | cantilevers loaded at the star's mid-plane, sized to `SPRING_SIGMA_Y_PA` or their §50 floors; in free space the stack is one `CLEAR_MARGIN` between layers, so L = margin + stock/2 for both (the fold re-sizes them at its real lengths) |
| r1 | the smallest orbit at which the Geneva's floors hold (`floorsHold`: a lock window, a hub round the star's bore) with the pin and stud sized at THAT orbit's load, and the star's tips clear the finger's own hub by a margin |
| the choice | of every (m, n) with a closed slot, the one whose plan reaches least far from the cone's axis: the pair's centre distance plus the star's far edge from the finger's axis |
