## §304 — LEGO build instructions, landing 3: a printable booklet per build for US Letter and A4

Third landing of the private roadmap's §297 scoping (landing 1 is §300's parts list, §301/§303 its BrickLink
catalogue and colours, landing 2 is §302's step order). The owner chose a **printable booklet** over an on-screen
viewer, **BrickLink** as the purchase catalogue, and **US Letter and A4** both. `booklet.html?cell=<id>&gen=&paper=letter|a4`
(`?mode=` for the keyless works and the alarm) lays out one build as a booklet: a cover, the parts list, the §302
steps as cards, a closing page; Print, then Save as PDF, gives pages that fit the chosen paper exactly. All eight builds
ship in this landing, both generations. Display only: no placement, `.ldr` line, colour, step or probe of landings 1-2
moved; `technic.html` gained one link per parts list.

**MODELLED, not built.** The booklet describes a model nobody has assembled. It says so on the cover, on the closing
page, beside every step that adds no part (a rubber band, the gong, the escapement's analogue) and in the footer of every
page. It never says a build works, was tested or was simulated; the forces behind the fusee, the alarm and the
escapement are POSED and the cover and closing page say so, and each band step says it again beside the band.
The escapement is "an analogue, not the Swiss lever" on its own step, and the alarm's fly "is not the steel anchor".
7785 and 7861 (unofficial 2026 gears) are flagged on their callout pip, on their step and on the parts list.

### What is on a page

| page | holds |
|---|---|
| cover | the build's title, its generation and paper, the finished model as modelled, the counts (parts, steps, colours, pages), the modelled-not-built box, a legend of the drawing grammar, the LDraw attribution |
| parts list | the §300/§301 lines, a picture, the quantity, the LDraw id and title, the colour, BrickLink item, colour id and element codes, the line's flags, then the "not in the model" block and the honesty text |
| step cards | a chapter band (the §302 chapters), then cards: the step number and a callout of exactly the parts the step adds (a picture and "×N" each), a picture of the build so far with the step's parts in their colours and everything earlier pale, the title, the text, the marks |
| closing | the whole build, the honesty text, what a red page means, the attribution |

The drawing grammar: **pale** parts are built, **coloured** parts are this step's; a new part hidden behind a built one is
shown again as a **tinted shadow** (a second pass whose depth test passes only behind what is already drawn); a **black
arrow** is the way in (the direction the validator's chosen path started along, else the step's first declared
direction); an **orange arc** round a gear is "turn it until its teeth mesh" (a circle in the plane of the gear, on its
pitch radius plus a standoff, drawn from its axle and tooth count); a **sub-assembly** is a tinted, dashed card ("off the
model") whose last card says which step lowers it in, and that step ("Place the sub-assembly") shows the unit going into
the model; a step that adds no part (a rubber band) is a text card with a generic diagram that says "where: not in the
model"; a **red box** is a step the check could not clear.

### How the flagged steps look on paper (TODO 264's closed inventory)

Two kinds of step are red. **A step whose part the swept insertion could not put in** (a hub boss over a brick, a
friction pin on a sideways path, the release disc's beams in the fly's, a flank over the tolerance; TODO 264's `FLAGGED`
inventory) and **a step that completes a pair of solids standing in each other at rest** (§302's `OVERLAPS`: the
balance's crossbar through a mass gear, a keyless axle against its neighbour's gear, the stem's joiner, the fusee's pawl
arm, a peg axle and the detent pawl's 8T in the baseplate; the generator reads them off the placed pose beside the sweep).
Both carry a card with a heavy red border and the reason: "NOT CLEARED. The swept-insertion check found no path that puts part 32062 (Technic Axle Pin) in
without touching part 3811 (Baseplate 32 x 32). It reaches 2.04 LDU into part 3811 where 1.78 LDU is allowed. As
modelled, this step may need a part or its neighbour changed before it can be built (TODO 264)." An overlap says "TWO SOLIDS IN EACH OTHER ... part
3708 stands 1.6 LDU inside part 3648 ... this step cannot be completed as drawn". A part the check found only with the
meshed gears turned, or in two moves, says that too. A booklet whose verdicts belong to another plan
than the one it prints marks every part step "NOT CHECKED" and the probe refuses it; the string, laid by hand, says
the sweep does not cover it.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| rows 1-4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | the four gear-train books are clean; row 4 has one gear that goes in in two moves (marked, not red) |
| keyless and motion works (wind; set has 8 red) | 7 of 40 | 5 | 2 | a 16T gear on an 8T gear, a 14T gear against a Technic brick, the rack against the 28T double bevel gear, a half bush against the same gear, the changeover catch against a 12-long axle; an axle in a joiner (1.17 LDU) and in a 24T gear (1.6) |

| alarm (armed; ringing has 8, off 5) | 6 of 59 | 6 | 0 | pins and beams against rotors, bricks and each other, a 16T and an 8T gear against beams and a pin (12 readings) |
| escapement and balance | 3 of 30 | 2 | 1 | an axle against a brick, a liftarm against an 8T gear; the balance's crossbar through a mass gear (4.31 LDU, the deepest overlap) |

20 of the eight default books' 216 steps are red (9 percent). Each is a finding about the MODEL, not a statement that
the others are safe. The 22 flagged readings in the four flagged books' default modes are mostly by-a-hair: the
excess over the allowance has median 0.16 LDU (0.06 mm), 19 of the 22 are within 0.3 LDU and the largest is 0.78
LDU (0.31 mm); the deepest reading in absolute terms is 2.18 LDU, a beam into a rotor. The overlaps are not hairs: 1.17 to 4.31 LDU.

A4 is narrower and taller than Letter (697.7 by 998.5 CSS px of content against 720 by 932), so the same cards fall on
different pages: three cards fit either, A4 gains slack and the parts list's rows break at other places. The keyless works' ring modes (set, neutral) and the alarm's states (ringing, off) are their own books

Step pages hold three cards (the derivation above); a callout of more than four parts grows its card instead.

### Instruments

`tools/probe-technic-booklet.mjs` (pure Node, about 20 s): coverage (every step in exactly one flow item, in the plan's
order), callouts recounted independently from the placements (the cord one string) and summing to the BOM line for
line, frames holding every vertex of the step's new parts and its arrows, ghosts equal to what was built before, the
closing picture equal to the placements, the flow inside both papers' content boxes, the three-up derivation, the
marks against the generated verdicts and §302's inventory, the words, the verdicts' signatures. 14 controls, all seen to
fail: a part dropped from a callout, a step drawn with the step before's parts, a frame shrunk off its parts, a closing
picture missing a part, a page overfilled, A4's pages judged on Letter's box, two steps swapped, a planted "works", a
missing modelled-not-built, the flagged marker removed, a marker with no reason, verdicts of another plan, no verdicts,
the unofficial flag removed. `tools/smoke-technic-booklet.mjs` (Chromium, SwiftShader): sheets the paper's size with
nothing past the box, every step once and in order, the sheets equal to `paginate` over the measured heights, every
picture non-blank and drawn again equal to its spec, the new parts differing from the pale view, the PDF with as many
pages as sheets at 612x792 pt or 595x842 pt, the chrome gone in print, the flagged markers equal to the validator's, a
390 px screen without sideways scroll; its controls: a step drawn without its new parts, the picture compared with
the step before's, a blanked picture, an overfilled sheet, the paper changed under the sheets, the flagged markers removed.
`node tools/booklet-flags.mjs --check` re-derives the verdicts (ten minutes; not CI). The existing technic probes
are unchanged and re-run (below).

**What the battery cannot see:** all of it. `booklet.html` and `src/technic-booklet*.js` are imported by nothing, and
`index.html`'s module graph reaches `layout.js` and none of the technic modules; `battery.yml` now lists the page among
the files it ignores.

### Shipping the page

`booklet.html` is an ADOPTED, noindex document like `technic.html`: `stamp-release.mjs` stamps and precaches it,
`build-pages.mjs` marks it noindex, `payload.sh` ships it with no change (it is an exclude list), `offline-check.mjs` copies
it and now BOOTS it offline (the module graph, the LDraw pack and three.js all have to be in the precache for a sheet to
stand). The page's library URL is spelled in the document, not in a module, because the stamper versions quoted
`./vendor/` URLs in documents and `from '...'` specifiers in modules only: a URL in a module would ship unstamped and
fall out of the offline set (found while writing it). `offline.yml`'s path filter did not list the technic page or its
modules, so landings that add precached modules never ran the job that counts them; it lists them now.

### What the scoping said, and what was measured

- "~130-170 steps in all (estimate: unmeasured)": the eight default books carry **216 steps** (208 that build something,
  the rest a band's note, the finished-sub-assembly lowering and each book's closing page), **111 Letter pages and 107
  A4 pages**. The scoping's cost for route C (12-18 engineer-days, "a layout engine is a second product") was an
  over-estimate for this repo: the booklet is a pure data module (about 360 lines), a three.js drawer (about 80), a DOM
  module (about 300) and a page (about 180), because the §302 plan already carried the chapters, the sub-assemblies, the turn angles and
  the directions, and §300's pictures already drew a part. What took the time was the honesty (the flagged steps, the
  overlaps) and the two papers.
- "A swept-insertion validator ... the page replays it": the page cannot run the sweep (three-mesh-bvh, minutes of Node), so
  the verdicts are generated and signed. A mismatch is visible, not silent.
- "Turn-to-mesh arrow for the solved phi": the arrow is an arc round the gear that says "turn until the teeth mesh" with
  no sense; phi is the modelled shaft angle already in the step's text. A builder turns either way.
- "Page break logic derived from the rendered content": done by measuring each card at the content width and flowing the
  heights through one pure `paginate`. The pure layout (which the probe uses) estimates heights on purpose high and the
  smoke holds it never low.
- Not asked, found: `offline.yml`'s path filter listed neither technic page nor their modules, so §300-§302 added six
  precached modules without moving the count `offline-check.mjs` asserts (84 at §294, 96 now: six earlier, six here); and a
  module-spelled `./vendor/` URL would have shipped unstamped (the stamper versions the documents' quoted URLs and the
  modules' `from` specifiers only).

### Left, named

- Picture fidelity is three.js's flat-shaded LDraw geometry, not LEGO's renders; no zoom bubble for a meshing detail.
