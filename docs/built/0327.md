## §327 — The form rectangle reaches the mainspring drum

**Found by:** `?alarm=0&case=fitted` — a first form boot (no `?formrect=`) cut the plates to a box the build's own fit
then said it did not hold. The boot printed `the movement's counted metal stands -13.523 u inside the fitted it was
cut to`, and measured per part the **Mainspring drum stood 10.18 u outside** the cut outline (the Chain with it).

**Cause.** `formPlate` (`layout.js`, `solveKeyless`) is the rectangle a form case's first boot is cut to, and it was
built from the `P` stations plus three keyless terms. The drum is not a `P` station: `main.js` places it after the
solve (`drumPos`) and only holds it inside the ROUND plate radius (TODO 207's `DRUM_R_ACTUAL` bound). So the fit
(`FORM_FOOTPRINT`, measured off the built meshes) always counted the drum and the guess box never did — every
`?case=` kind without a carried `?formrect=` started short of it.

**Change.** `solveKeyless` takes an optional `drum: { dist, r }` and adds that circle, under the same rim margin as a
station, at the place `main.js` puts it. The direction is ONE law now (`drumDirOf`, `DRUM_OUTWARD_BLEND`), which
`main.js`' `drumPos` calls too, so the placement and the rectangle's reach cannot part (the 0.45 blend was written in
`main.js` alone). `main.js` passes `dist = FUSEE_DRUM_DIST` and `r = DRUM_WRAP_R + CHAIN_END_R_OUT` — the outermost
metal, the chain coil on the wall, the same radius TODO 207's plate bound uses. Plain numbers, because
`tools/form-layout.mjs` serialises `keylessInputs`.

**Measured** (`?alarm=0&case=fitted`, first boot): the box's top edge moved from y 25.85 to 41.20 and the drum, chain
and set-up work no longer stand outside the cut outline (drum was −10.18). Parts still outside are the winding stem
and its levers, which pass through the case wall and are not counted (`FORM_COUNTED`).

**Not moved (and what is not yet proven).** The round build never reads `formPlate`, and every validated point carries
its own `formRectSpec` (`src/validated-configs.js`), so those are cut to the carried shape and not to this fallback.
`tools/gong-seats.mjs` measures at a `?formrect=` fixed point (its own header), so its tables should not move either;
`tools/gong-seats.mjs`/`gong-bench-data.mjs` `--check` and the battery are what confirm that, and were not run
locally. A form build with NO carried rectangle (`?case=rect` alone, the free outline's fallback polygon) now starts
larger by design; the fit still decides the final shape.
