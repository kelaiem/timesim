## §331 — The convex form case: its band bored for its tubes, and cut without slivers

Out of the owner's ask (2026-11-03) to profile the hosted battery and then optimize `form-rect`'s point sweep, the push's critical path.

### What the profile found

On the push `af560fd9`, the point tier (13.6 min) was the battery's critical path, and `form-rect` alone took 815 s of it. Solo in the dev container it took 1,104 s, of which `undeclaredClearance` was 676 s. Timing every exact clearance call by unit pair showed **~93% of that was pairs against the Case unit**, at 50–850 ms a call. The round case's Case pairs, in `designed`, cost ~9 ms.

The cause was triangle shape, not count. The rectangular case had half the round case's triangles, but `form.loop` emits a straight side as one edge:
- each side became one wall quad;
- earcut joined points a side apart across the caps;
- so `caseMiddle` carried 420 triangles with an edge over 20 u (up to 90.5 u, the case's width), and `caseBack` 210. The round case carries none.

A triangle that long has a bounding box across half the case, which no BVH query near it can prune.

### What that exposed

Cutting those faces short made `undeclaredClearance` report **`Case ⇄ Keyless works`, the winding stem against the case middle, at 0**. On `main` the battery read the pair 2.289 u clear. An exact edge–triangle count on `main` finds **96 crossings**: the form band was "not bored for the tubes" (this file's header, kept as declared CSG debt), and every crown stem passed through solid band metal. The slivers had blinded `meshClearance` to it, and `form-rect` was a listed validated configuration on that false reading. That is filed as **TODO 282**. The round case bores its band and reads 0 crossings.

So this landing fixes the metal, not the reading.

### What shipped

- **`densifyLoop`** splits every loop edge longer than the loop's median edge (on a rectangle, the corners' arc chord) into equal collinear pieces. The outline, every mitre offset of it and every ray exit are unchanged; only the triangulation is finer. `formCaseLoops`' outer loop takes it, so every ring and sweep does.
- **`annulusPrism`** caps the back's two annulus prisms on a convex form by stitching their two loops, as a loft joins two contours. earcut, given the densified loops, fanned the collinear runs into longer slivers (2,364 over 20 u when tried).
- **`boredBand`** builds the band on a convex form cell by cell between the outer loop and its index-aligned bore. Both loops are split exactly where each tube's strip crosses either wall: the strip is the sleeve's outer radius `ap = d/2 + TUBE_WALL` round its line, the `tubeAt` expression. The z window `z ± ap` is taken out of every cell the strip covers. This is the round case's BORED-sector rule on the form's loops. Radial faces stand only where neighbouring cells' metal differs, so the body stays closed. A window reaching past the band's own wall warns.
- **`POINT_COSTS`' `form-rect` row** is re-measured: 426, which puts `designed` first in the LPT order.
- **The free outline is untouched.** Its band is still unbored (**TODO 283**).
