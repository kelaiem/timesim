## §75 — Untether the mainspring drum: its azimuth as a spec dimension — PARTIAL: step 1 shipped

Out of the owner's ask (2026-11-02): could the mainspring drum fold more tightly about the centre, for a narrower watch? The drum
stands beside the fusee (`P.barrel`), aimed by `drumDirOf` (`layout.js`): perpendicular to the stem, blended OUTWARD by
`DRUM_OUTWARD_BLEND` (0.45) so the chain's span clears the train, at the centre distance `FUSEE_DRUM_DIST` (`FUSEE_R_LARGE +
DRUM_R_ACTUAL + 2.5`). Step 1 makes the azimuth a spec dimension and measures what turning it buys. It buys nothing yet, and the
record says why.

### What shipped

- **`?drumaz=` / `SPEC.drumAzDeg`** — the drum's ABSOLUTE azimuth about the fusee in degrees, movement frame (P's x/y,
  counter-clockwise from +x), normalised to [0, 360). Absent, `drumDirOf` keeps its blend and the build is unchanged: the default
  fingerprint is **1995847421** before and after, boot silent. `drumDirOf(uWind, vPerp, sideSign, azDeg)` takes the override, and
  both its readers pass it — `main.js`' `drumPos` and §327's form-rectangle drum circle — so the placement and the rectangle's reach
  to it stay one law. The gap (the `2.5`) is NOT a key yet: it is a term of `FUSEE_DRUM_DIST`, which the fusee's torque law reads,
  so moving it re-cuts the fusee and is the equalisation gate's business.
- **TODO 207's assert, under a moved drum.** `DRUM_R_ACTUAL` is the plate's bound measured along the DEFAULT direction. A moved drum
  keeps that size, so with `?drumaz=` set the assert holds only the fit (its chain coil one `CLEAR_MARGIN` inside `plateR`), not
  "as large as the plate allows".
- **`__clock.drum`** — the drum's centre, direction, azimuth, centre distance and coil reach, in the frame `?drumaz=` takes.
- **`__clock.windArrest.candidateScan`** — §47's beak scan for EVERY pad candidate it tried (its trace and reject counts), where the
  record used to keep only the chosen candidate's. It is how the blocker below was found.
- **`tools/probe-75-drum-az.mjs`** (a report) — per azimuth, optionally fits the free outline (`--fit`, restoring
  `src/free-outlines.js` afterwards), boots, and runs `inspection`, `clearances` and `undeclaredClearance` restricted to the drum, the
  chain and the set-up work, printing BATTERY's own gate rows for those units.

### Finding 1: on the round build the drum does not size the plate

`plateR` is the tightest circle over the `P` stations' outlines plus the keyless floors; the drum is not in it. TODO 207 then grows
the drum until its coil meets the rim. Measured on the default build (each term with the 5 u rim margin): the folded minute wheel
**41.516 u** (binds), the stem bushing's foot 40.619, the setting wheel 40.060, the balance 39.526, the fusee station 36.507. So a
drum turned inward leaves the round plate where it is (and under TODO 207's law would only grow). A narrower ROUND movement is the
keyless cluster's question, not this entry's.

### Finding 3: what blocks 125–135° is the winding arrest, and no tuning of it opens a window

§47's arrest picks a pad azimuth (where the arriving coil reaches its band late in the wind) and then scans the parked beak outward
from the pad in `BEAK_SCAN_STEP` (0.02 rad) steps. The candidate scan, lever side first (`s` arm under 1.1·`R_PAD_ARM`, `c` chain in
the tab's band over the wind, `l` arm over 2.2·`R_PAD_ARM`, `n` throw outward, `.` legal):

- shipped drum: one candidate (pad 5°), `21s 3. 12c 69l | 11n 94l` — **a three-step (3.4°) legal window**, and the whole of the
  arrest's freedom;
- drum 125° or 135°: 69–73 candidates (pad 1–4.5° and 316–357°), every one `21s 15c 69l | 11n 94l`; the candidates at 223–304° fail
  earlier (the span crosses the pad arm).

Turning the drum turns the chain's departure; touch moves earlier (0.965 → 0.917) and the chain's band at the tab grows by exactly the
three steps that were legal. Measured on throwaway builds, none kept:
- **the lug's proudness** (`LUG_OUTER`, TODO 51's lever) +0.05 to +0.3 u: the trace does not move (the stud's floor follows the lug);
  past +0.3 the bracket and hub reach inside the lug's orbit;
- **the ratio band's floor** 1.1 → 1.0 / 0.9 / 0.8: `19s 17c`, `18s 18c`, `16s 20c` — every step freed from `s` is chain;
- the tab's z band is TODO 51's refused relief (the departure corridor then covers every beak azimuth), and the far side of the pad
  is chain-free but throws the beak OUTWARD (`n`).

So at 125–135° the chain fills the beak's whole reachable arc on the side the lever throws inward. Winning the 3–6% needs a different
arrest — the beak on the chain-free side with a reversing link — which is a new mechanism, designed in a line (P0/P1) before it is
folded. It is filed in the roadmap as §329. Until then the shipped drum direction is the only clean one on the lands
preview, and `?drumaz=` is a dimension for measurement, not a smaller case.
