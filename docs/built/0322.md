### Why the escape wheel left the plate

"Round the opening to show more of the escape wheel" cannot be done while the
plate is the wheel's bearing: §148's chaton needs its counterbore (2.33) and
two or three screw seats on a 3.12 circle, each 1.03 across, so the plate must
keep a disc of **4.16 + margin** about a wheel of **4.5** — a rounding would
show the teeth's outer 0.19 and nothing else, and it would have to island the
boss on webs, which §148 withdrew. The bearing moved: the escape wheel's upper
pivot runs in the ESCAPE COCK's stone, and the plate keeps no boss at its
axis. That is Glashütte practice and this repository's own `MECH_GRAPH`
comment, and it is why the `'Escape wheel'` → `'Three-quarter plate'` support
row is a cock row now. The fourth wheel keeps its chaton, its collar and its
support row in the plate.
