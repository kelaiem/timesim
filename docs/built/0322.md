### One cut, three more tenants, and a bridge

**Why the fourth wheel could not be a window.** A §62 window owes every other
opening a `TQ_LAND_MIN` strip, the kidney cut included, and the fourth wheel's
disc overlaps the escape wheel's (8.63 + 4.5 across a 9.24 centre distance):
no strip fits between a window that frames the fourth wheel and a cut that
frames the escape wheel. So the kidney cut takes them. It is the one
star-shaped table about the balance axis that `inCutClearance`, the pillar
seats, the chaton seating and the stop-work solve all read, and every new
tenant VOTES its swept silhouette into it the way the fork always has
(`openworkVotes`): the escape wheel and the fourth wheel as their tip circles
(revolvers — TODO 7's class, answered by construction), the heart cam the
same, and the reset hammer swung about its own pivot over `HAMMER_SWING_RAD`
from the seated pose to the retracted one, read in the group's own frame
because the group stands at rotation 0 until the first tick writes it (the
fork's trap, §62). The bridge votes the moment it stands and again on the
balance cock's reveal pass, which re-finishes the raw table once.

**Why the escape wheel left the plate.** "Round the opening to show more of
the escape wheel" cannot be done while the plate is the wheel's bearing: §148's
chaton needs its counterbore (2.33) and two or three screw seats on a 3.12
circle, each 1.03 across, so the plate must keep a disc of **4.16 + margin**
about a wheel of **4.5** — a rounding would show the teeth's outer 0.19 and
nothing else, and it would have to island the boss on webs, which §148
withdrew. The bearing moved: the escape wheel's upper pivot runs in the
FOURTH WHEEL BRIDGE's second stone, and the plate keeps no boss at its axis.
That is the Glashütte escape-and-fourth cock this repository's `MECH_GRAPH`
comment has described since the three train bridges went, and it is why the
`'Fourth wheel'`/`'Escape wheel'` → `'Three-quarter plate'` support rows are
bridge rows now.
