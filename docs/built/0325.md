## §325 — The free outline: an asymmetric, concave form case fitted to the movement's metal

Out of the owner's request: "an aggressively fitted non-symmetric case … using the movement that has the smallest
possible footprint, possibly omitting the alarm complication and gong". Measured first, on the fitted build: the
smallest CONVEX case (the metal's hull grown by the rim margin) is 878 mm² with the alarm, 766 mm² without it, and
598 mm² once the frame (pillars, cocks, bridges) is re-sited to the going works; §317's fitted tonneau cuts 1068.5.
The owner chose the order — the shape first on today's movement, then a movement with no alarm (landing 2), then the
frame re-sited (landing 3) — and the shape's freedom: free, concave included. This is landing 1. `?case=free` is the
sixth case kind ("Free outline" in both case-shape selects, every locale). The round build does not move.

### The shape (`fitFreeCase`, layout.js)

No construction at all: no box, no mirror, no barrel. The outline is the counted metal's own plan, in four steps:

1. **The lug ends.** A strap needs two flat lands, so each end is a straight flat of the strap's need either side
   of a STRAP AXIS x = c (`FITTED_FLAT_MIN`, §317's law: the lug pair's outboard reach, 11.000 mm), at the grown
   metal's reach across the strip the flat and its fillets occupy (|x − c| ≤ need + rc) — and nowhere else: beyond
   that strip the case may rise past the flat into shoulders beside the lugs, the integrated-lug case's form. It
   gained little here (976.2 → 974.3 mm² at c 5): the 22 mm strap is nearly as wide as the movement.
2. **Grown by the rim margin M**, each counted mesh's own plan hull. A concave outline can pass between two meshes,
   so the one hull of them all (every other kind's test) is no longer the worst case of what it encloses.
3. **Closed by FREE_RC** = CASE_CLEAR + 2·CASE_BAND_T (7.917 u, 3 mm): every inlet narrower than 2·rc filled, every
   re-entrant turn no tighter than rc. The band's outside is this outline grown CASE_CLEAR + CASE_BAND_T
   (`formCaseLoops`), where a re-entrant round r becomes r − that grow, so the cut's round is that grow plus one band
   thickness and the band's outside turns no tighter than the band is thick rather than to a cusp.
4. **Opened by the margin's round**, eroded M and grown back: the closing's contour is the boundary of a union of
   disks about the raster's outside cells and carries small convex cusps where it stair-steps; the outline's
   convex turns are meant to be the margin's round. Kept only while it holds the metal.

It is computed on a FIELD: the sets rasterised at FREE_H (0.1 u), their distance transforms exact on the cell
centres (Felzenszwalb's lower envelope), each step a level of one, the outline a marching-squares contour simplified
to FREE_SIMPLIFY (0.004 u). The raster is taken on the safe side at every step and the outline is then held to the
metal EXACTLY (`freeWorst`: every metal edge against every outline edge, every pin its own need) and pulled in by
whatever excess the raster left, so the raster is how the outline is found, never the evidence that it holds.

**Pins.** A box-fitted kind leaves the build's other distance checks slack; the free outline hugs the metal, so
every check that holds a distance inside the outline is a pin of the fit, at its own need (`FREE_PINS`, main.js):
every plate opening's circle and land (`formHolds`), the reserve and seconds wells' walls inside the dial track,
the late base-plate holes, the hammer spring's stud, the strike opening's land, the gong's wire and block. Each check
still warns on its own reading. §311's track check was conservative (it warned whenever the form rail came inside
the round rail the wells were solved against); it now holds each well to the form rail itself, by the solver's own
rule, which the fitted kinds never tripped either way.

### The strap axis and the fixed point (`tools/free-outline.mjs`, `src/free-outlines.js`)

The outline is no box a `?formrect=` can carry, so it is GENERATED per movement into `src/free-outlines.js`, keyed
by the movement's configuration key, and the build cuts its plates, dial and case to the row (a movement with no row
is cut to the solvers' box and says to run the tool). The tool boots `?case=free`, reads the counted metal's plan
hulls and pins off `__clock.formFootprint.free`, and searches the strap axis in Node with the build's own law (a scan
every 2.5 u over the metal's middle, then golden section to 0.05 u: the least area). The counted metal follows the
outline (a pillar seated on the plate, a stud planted in it), so it boots again on the new row until the axis and the
area stop moving and the build holds the metal with no §325 warning: five rounds from the stand-in box.
`--check` re-derives; `--fresh` starts from the box rather than the committed row.

The build re-fits the measured metal at the row's axis on every free boot and warns when the area has moved.

### The case's rings

§289's bezel and every ring inside the band (`formCaseLoops`) were the outer loop's MITRE offsets — a vertex per
vertex, which the sweep needs. On the free outline that folds, at every flat corner and along the right side's bump:
the outline is a polyline, and grown G it turns at each vertex on an arc of radius G, so a mitre offset deeper than
G (the bezel's opening is G + 1 mm) runs backwards there whatever the curve's own curvature. Coarsening and smoothing
the loop were measured and do not cure it. The free kind's rings are its own EXACT offsets instead — one signed field
of the outline over the band's depths, a contour per depth — each carried onto the outer loop's vertices by nearest
point, which between two exact offsets never runs backwards (at a corner of the deeper one several points land on
the corner: zero-area triangles, never inverted ones). §289's check reads the free kind as "no edge reversed, no
crossing" rather than "convex", which no concave outline is.

The metal stands 5.008 u inside it (need 5); the lug ends run ±29.18 u (need 29.03). Booted with its row, the build
prints exactly the two warnings every form build carries (§186's plate seat, §261's depth).

`tools/probe-325-free-case.mjs` holds the toolkit (the transform exact against brute force, an offset of a circle a
circle, the inset signed by the polygon's own inside), the fit on the measured metal (`tools/data/probe-325-metal.json`,
`--refresh` re-measures it: it holds, it is tight — pulled in 0.02 u it refuses the metal — the ends are straight and
run their need, it is concave and simple, the committed row is the fit, the axis is the bracketed minimum, it encloses
less than the fitted tonneau), the rings (every depth to the opening an exact, unfolded offset; the control: §289's
mitre folds on the same loop at 146 edges) and the dial frame.

### Open, named

- **The gong seat is the fitted tonneau's**, borrowed (`SEAT_KIND`): the free outline is fitted around the seated
  gong (its wire and block are pins). A seat solved for the free outline itself is moot by landing 2, which drops the
  gong with the alarm.
- **No battery has swept a free build** (TODO 158): like every form configuration it is UNVERIFIED until a
  `SPEC_POINTS` row runs.
- **What sets the ends.** The two flats sit on two small parts at the movement's top and bottom reach, pillars and
  screws sited for the round plate. Landing 3 re-sites the frame.
- **Boot cost.** The fit and the band's field add ~2 s to a free boot on this container, handed back on breathe()'s
  budget through no numbered seam (the boot bar's table is the default build's, §317's precedent).
