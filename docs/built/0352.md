## §352 — The escapement cell carries the going train's balance: the rating nut on its rod, so its parts list is the balance the drive and the knife test are about

The owner asked for a LEGO-only kit to build the escapement and balance for the knife-edge free-decay test (§323's
landing 0). The cell that stands alone, `technic.html?cell=escapement`, was §287's balance. The going train's balance,
the one §316's drive solves and the decay test will calibrate, carries §316's RATING NUT. So the cell's parts list was
not the balance the figures are about. The owner chose to make the cell carry the train's balance.

**What changed.** One function, `frameEscapementCell()` in `src/technic-drive.js`, frames the cell:
- §287's escapement and balance;
- plus the rating nut at `TECHNIC_DRIVE.nutV` (−17.17 mm): an angle connector #6 (32014) on the bob axle, a 4L rod
  (3705) standing in it, and a bush (3713) as the nut.

`frameEscapement` already builds the nut for the train (`rating`), so nothing about the nut is new. Carrying the
connector takes the bob axle from a 3L (4519) to a 4L (3705).

Every site that built the cell calls this function:
- the parts list (`buildOf` in `src/technic-bom.js`), and through it the booklet and the BrickLink wanted list;
- the page (`technic.html`);
- the parts-list and carousel probes.

That way the page, the list and the probes cannot frame different cells.

**What did not change.**
- **The pallets keep §287's setting (38.75°)**: a setting on a friction pin, not a part, and the cell's posed swing is
  traced at it. The train keeps §316's 37.25°.
- **§287's own probe still frames the bare balance**, its beat and lattice survey unchanged.

**The parts list:** 46 parts became 49. The 4519 is out; two more 3705, one 32014 (white) and one 3713 (red) are in.
It is the same list as the one-off wanted list the owner was sent before this landing.

**Whether the nut clears the cell's frame** is answered by the §302 swept insertion: every step of the cell's
regenerated booklet clears, the nut's step included (`src/technic-booklet-flags.js`, regenerated for the cell, its plan
signature moved), and `probe-technic-booklet` reads 0 flagged.

Modelled, not built: the rating nut's place is the drive's solve, and its grip on the rod is posed, as in §316.
