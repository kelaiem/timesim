## §314 — The arrangement is every build's default, the round movement's too

Out of the owner's call after §313: the round movement takes the form cases' arrangement, so the battery sweeps
the movement the cases are cut for. The arrangement was re-derived on the way, because the first whole
battery on §313's arrangement found it could not be that one.

**Why not §313's arrangement.** §313 swung the barrel to 180° (`barrelstep` 0), the alarm module to 60° and held
the reserve at 12 on six idlers. Its first full sweep as the default read eight FORBIDDEN pairs, the first two
the fusee and its chain through the third wheel. That one is the layout, not a part: `solveLayout` puts the centre
a stage step from the barrel and the third a wedge round from it, and the fusee's swept cone (r 5.53) and the third
wheel (r 9.82) need 15.50 between their axes. At step 0 they stand 13.59 apart. The designed −35° stands 20.51
apart. Re-rooting the third wheel on the mirror side clears it, but grows the plate from 45.6 to 48.9 and drops
the strike group out of its annulus, so that was refused. The barrel is stepped instead: −9° is the first whole
degree that clears, and the owner chose −10°, a degree in hand.

**The arrangement at −10°**, scanned per idler count and module turn (the boot's own refusals):

| idlers | module | reads |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 50° | the alarm link's measured site refused by the reserve train |
| 2 | 45°, 50°, 55° | clean (once the arming fixed point is quoted) |
| 4 | 50°, 55° | clean |
| 4 | 60° | link −0.032 |
| 4 | 65° | refused |

Two idlers, the fewest parts, at 50°, the middle of their window. `ARRANGEMENT` is `{ barrelStepDeg: −10,
rsvAzDeg: 0, alarmModAzDeg: 50, rsvIdlers: 2 }`. The arming fixed point is quoted at its key (5.2249 mN). A named
idler count that admits no train at all (e.g. six at −10°) now warns and builds the two-stage train rather than
throwing on a null one.

**The default.** `FORM_ARRANGEMENT` is `ARRANGEMENT` (layout.js) and applies to every build, round or form,
unless the URL names one of its keys (`barrelstep`, `rsvaz`, `alarmmod`, `rsvidl`) or carries
`?arrangement=designed`, which builds the designed train exactly as before. The marker is read by `SPEC` and is
NOT a spec field, so it never enters a configKey: `?arrangement=designed` keys exactly as the old identity did,
and `?arrangement=designed&balstep=60` as `?balstep=60` did. The index roster carries it (`arrangement`), so the
share link and saved variants travel with it. A reconfigure knob now applies on top of the arrangement.

**The jumper-site scan's order.** Its stages were ordered by measured milliseconds, which made a boot's work
depend on the machine, and the boot bar (§267) strayed 18.1 points on CI. They are ordered now by refusals per
unit of COUNTED geometric work (the BVH short-circuits, the rotor clearances, the inside tests), so a boot visits
the same seams every time and `src/boot-progress.js` regenerates.

**The battery.** The identity spec point is the arrangement (silent since §313). The designed train is a new
spec point, `designed` (`?arrangement=designed`), silent and swept. The designed-train variants keep their
boots as the builds they always were (`arrangement=designed&…` prefixed on every row but the identity,
`reconf=1` and `arrangement-308`, which names its keys), but no longer sweep: a point is swept over the units its
build changes against the default, and against the arrangement each variant changes most of the movement, a
near-full sweep apiece. Their six validated rows were withdrawn (`tools/validated-configs.mjs --drop`, new: a
claim may always be withdrawn by hand; only adding one needs a whole run's points file), and the identity row
re-rendered at the arrangement's key. The `designed` row joins from the first whole run's points file.

**Probes.** `probe-313-reserve-at-12.mjs` reads the bare URL as the arrangement and holds it to the arrangement
spelled out (one fingerprint), the designed train at `?arrangement=designed`. Its controls moved with the
arrangement: four idlers now WORK at 50°, so the four-idler control is gone, and no idlers at −10° is the link's
refusal rather than §313's missing cap bearing. `probe-308-barrel-swing.mjs`'s
round build R is `?arrangement=designed`.
