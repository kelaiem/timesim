## §314 — The arrangement is every build's default, the round movement's too

Out of the owner's call after §313: the round movement takes the form cases' arrangement, so the battery sweeps
the movement the cases are cut for. The arrangement was re-derived on the way, because the first whole
battery on §313's arrangement found it could not be that one, and then re-derived again, because the second
found the first fix one collision short.

**Why not §313's arrangement.** §313 swung the barrel to 180° (`barrelstep` 0), the alarm module to 60° and held
the reserve at 12 on six idlers. Its first full sweep as the default read eight FORBIDDEN pairs, the first two
the fusee and its chain through the third wheel. That one is the layout, not a part: `solveLayout` puts the centre
a stage step from the barrel and the third a wedge round from it, and the fusee's swept cone (r 5.53) and the third
wheel (r 9.82) need 15.50 between their axes. At step 0 they stand 13.59 apart; the designed −35° stands 20.51
apart. Re-rooting the third wheel on the mirror side clears it, but grows the plate from 45.6 to 48.9 and drops
the strike group out of its annulus, so that was refused. The barrel is stepped instead.

**Why −20° and not −10°.** −9° is the first whole degree that clears the cone, and the owner first chose −10°.
The next whole battery found the GREAT WHEEL (r 15.65) through the third wheel's arbor (r 0.50) and its pinion
(r 1.63, standing 0.06 over the wheel's plane, inside the margin, so it counts in plan): 17.43 between the axes
clears both, which the step first reaches at −19°. The owner chose −20°, a degree in hand.

**The arrangement at −10°**, scanned per idler count and module turn (the boot's own refusals), which is where
the idler count was chosen:

| idlers | module | reads |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 50° | the alarm link's measured site refused by the reserve train |
| 2 | 45°, 50°, 55° | clean (once the arming fixed point is quoted) |
| 4 | 50°, 55° | clean |
| 4 | 60° | link −0.032 |
| 4 | 65° | refused |

At −20° two idlers keep the link's site with the module at 45° and 50°. Two, the fewest parts, at 50°.
`ARRANGEMENT` is `{ barrelStepDeg: −20, rsvAzDeg: 0, alarmModAzDeg: 50, rsvIdlers: 2 }`. The arming fixed point is
quoted at its key (5.2248 mN). A named idler count that admits no train at all now warns and builds the
two-stage train rather than throwing on a null one.

**The default.** `ARRANGEMENT` (layout.js) applies to every build, round or form, unless the URL names one of
its keys (`barrelstep`, `rsvaz`, `alarmmod`, `rsvidl`) or carries `?arrangement=designed`, which builds the
designed train exactly as before. The marker is read by `SPEC` and is NOT a spec field, so it never enters a
configKey: `?arrangement=designed` keys exactly as the old identity did, and `?arrangement=designed&balstep=60`
as `?balstep=60` did. The index roster carries it (`arrangement`), so the share link and saved variants travel
with it. A reconfigure knob now applies on top of the arrangement.

**The form cases' gong.** At the 50° module no bar seats inside §307's 40 mm cap: re-solved by
`tools/gong-seats.mjs`, the rect's and cushion's bar candidates read 40.15–40.75 mm across the short side, and the
cushion's others had the hammer set the caseback skirt's floor. The owner chose the SPIRAL as a form case's

probe-310's controls, probe-313's form builds, the gong bench and its data follow. The `form-rect` sweep reads one
FORBIDDEN pair (the hammer's arm across the alarm pusher's reach) and one hairline, recorded in TODO 274 with

**The back envelope.** The fusee's let-down square turns with it, and at −20° its corners sweep a bin the build
pose leaves empty (swept 11.204 at r 15.6–15.7 against the plate's 10.314 declared): `BACK_SWEPT_REGIONS` carries
the annulus, derived from the axis's radius and the square's half-diagonal, at `LETDOWN_TOP`. The boot bar was
re-measured once more with the yoke lawed.

**The jumper-site scan's order.** Its stages were ordered by measured milliseconds, which made a boot's work
depend on the machine, and the boot bar (§267) strayed 18.1 points on CI. They are ordered now by refusals per
unit of COUNTED geometric work (the BVH short-circuits, the rotor clearances, the inside tests), so a boot visits
the same seams every time and `src/boot-progress.js` regenerates.

**The battery.** The identity spec point is the arrangement. The designed train is a spec point, `designed`
(`?arrangement=designed`), silent and swept. The designed-train variants keep their boots as the builds they
always were (`arrangement=designed&…` prefixed on every row but the identity, `reconf=1`, `route=channel` — whose
claim is that a refused route builds the identity — and `arrangement-308`, which names its keys), but no longer
sweep: a point is swept over the units its build changes against the default, and against the arrangement each
variant changes most of the movement, a near-full sweep apiece. Their six validated rows were withdrawn
(`tools/validated-configs.mjs --drop`, new: a claim may always be withdrawn by hand; only adding one needs a
whole run's points file), and the identity row re-rendered at the arrangement's key.

**Probes.** `probe-313-reserve-at-12.mjs` reads the bare URL as the arrangement and holds it to the arrangement
spelled out (one fingerprint), the designed train at `?arrangement=designed`. Its controls moved with the
arrangement: no idlers is the link's refusal rather than §313's missing cap bearing, and the four-idler
control is gone. `probe-308-barrel-swing.mjs`'s round build R is `?arrangement=designed`.
`probe-314-linkage-line.mjs` is new (above).
