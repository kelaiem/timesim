## §314 — The arrangement is every build's default, the round movement's too

Out of the owner's call after §313: the round movement takes the arrangement the form cases were cut for —
the barrel swung to 180°, the alarm module at 60°, the power reserve at 12 o'clock on six idlers — so the
movement the battery sweeps is the one the cases are cut for.

**The default.** `FORM_ARRANGEMENT` is `ARRANGEMENT` (layout.js) and applies to every build, round or form,
unless the URL names one of its keys (`barrelstep`, `rsvaz`, `alarmmod`, `rsvidl`) or carries
`?arrangement=designed`, which builds the designed train exactly as before. The marker is read by `SPEC` and is
NOT a spec field, so it never enters a configKey: `?arrangement=designed` keys exactly as the old identity did,
and `?arrangement=designed&balstep=60` as `?balstep=60` did. The index roster carries it (`arrangement`), so the
share link and saved variants travel with it. A reconfigure knob now applies on top of the arrangement.

**The battery.** The identity spec point is the arrangement (silent since §313). The designed train is a new
spec point, `designed` (`?arrangement=designed`), silent and swept. The designed-train variants keep their
boots as the builds they always were (`arrangement=designed&…` prefixed on every row but the identity,
`reconf=1` and `arrangement-308`, which names its keys), but no longer sweep: a point is swept over the units its
build changes against the default, and against the arrangement each variant changes most of the movement, a
near-full sweep apiece. Their six validated rows were withdrawn (`tools/validated-configs.mjs --drop`, new: a
claim may always be withdrawn by hand; only adding one needs a whole run's points file), and the identity row
re-rendered at the arrangement's key. The `designed` row joins from the first whole run's points file.

**Probes.** `probe-313-reserve-at-12.mjs` reads the bare URL as the arrangement and holds it to the arrangement
spelled out (one fingerprint), the designed train at `?arrangement=designed`. `probe-308-barrel-swing.mjs`'s
round build R is `?arrangement=designed`.
