## §348 — The build replay: the schematic view plays the build's placements and the searches that sited them

**The ask** (the owner, reading the fixed-placements audit): animate the placement in the schematic view. The audit had
counted only the global layout solve, which read as if little of the build were solved. In fact nearly every part searches
for its own site as it is built, against everything already placed: about 18 azimuth or bearing scans, 37 bisections and a
dozen named solvers in `main.js`, run once each, greedily, in source order. The replay shows that.

### What it records (`BUILD_LOG`, beside `registerLabel`)

- **The order**: `registerLabel` appends each unit as the build places it (65 on the default build).
- **The searches**, each tagged `atUnit` (how many units stood when it ran, which is where the replay plays it), and each
  logging only what it ALREADY judged, never a second judgement:
  - the balance's edge walk (`solveLayout`'s new `trace` argument: every angle `ok` judged, 410 on the default, mapped into
    the final frame after `P` is built, so no float of the layout moves);
  - the balance cock's seat scan (its `scanRows`: 360 bearings, the kept φ);
  - the pillar or land seats (every ring point `seatClearance` judged, 2,800 on the default; the kept seats are
    `pillarSeats` itself);
  - the escape cock's foot (its `landscape`: each bearing's nearest candidate, and the foot kept).
- Published as `__clock.buildLog`.

### What it plays (View panel, "Build replay")

It turns the schematic view on if it is off and flies to a plan view from the movement side (`goToPose`, the presets' own
0.9 s ease, a snap under reduced motion). Each unit's plan footprint (the convex hull of its metal, sampled) then appears in
build order, grey where it was placed and green where a search sited it. At each search's place in the order its candidates
fan out (clear in green, refused in red, the balance's keep-out circles in red) and the one it kept lights in brass, then
fades to a trace. The status line names the unit or the search and its candidate count. It ends by itself, or on a second
press, or when the schematic view is turned off.

**What keeps it out of the movement.** Nothing exists until the button is pressed. Every object is a `Line`, `LineLoop` or
`Points` (the tier's never-a-Mesh rule) flagged `userData.schematic`, so `collectUnits` prunes it from every sweep, and it
draws on a camera layer of its own (2), so the tier's proxies and occluders keep their layer and their state; the camera's
mask is restored on exit.

### Measured

The default build boots silent and its fingerprint is `main`'s (3711590044 on both). `tools/boot-progress.mjs --check`:
383 seams, unchanged. In Chromium the replay plays to its end with no console error, follows the searches in its status line
and restores the schematic layer. `tools/probe-chrome-coverage.mjs`: the five new strings ('Build replay', 'placed',
'searched', 'refused', 'chosen', one row each in all 26 tables) apply in every locale; the probe's only English is the
frame-time readout ("33.3 ms · 30 fps") in four locales, a live figure unrelated to this change.

### Not done

- Only four searches log. The others (the cap solve, the folds, the alarm link's seat, the stop-work site, the reserve
  bearings) play as placements until they log too: each is one `logSearch` call where it keeps its answer.
- The colours say placed or searched, not WHY a part stands where it does (a typed seed, a quoted length). That needs a
  per-unit annotation the build does not carry yet.
