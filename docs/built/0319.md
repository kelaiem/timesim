## §319 — Gong strike tolerance: the hammer rests anywhere the blow's tilt budget reaches

**Why.** §310's strike was one exact point. A side strike is the root of `|P − Q| = ρ` along the wire (the
hammer's pivot on the lifting lever's circle), and an end strike is the wire's end with the pivot built from
it, so a wire placed a unit or two off its exact station was simply not reachable and the solver dropped it.
The volume it protects is not that sensitive: measured on a straight 2 kHz bar, moving the contact inboard
from the free end costs 0.12 dB of f₁ per percent of the wire's length (0.62 dB at 5%, 1.3 dB at 10%).

**What is free, and what stays exact.** Not the lever. The circle, `tanLen`, the rest gap and every arm are
held to float noise exactly as before (the forbidden P3 resolutions are stretching an arm and opening a
contact). What the mechanism does not spend is the blow's TILT `α` off the wire's normal (a side strike) or
tangent (an end strike). The face stays one gap off the wire's surface on a line of action through the round
wire's axis, so the contact stays normal to the wire's surface; only the transverse drive loses `cos α`.
With the tilt a free coordinate, `g(s, α) = 0` is a curve, not a point: the contact slides along the wire as
the tilt changes, and a placement the untilted solve misses is reached.

**The budget, derived.** `STRIKE_TOL_DB = 0.5` is the f₁ level a seat may give up; the tilt that spends it is
`STRIKE_ALPHA_MAX = acos(10^(−0.5/20))`, 19.25°. The slide along the wire is bounded by the same budget
through the mode shape (0.12 dB a percent on the bar). A node strike (the purest rule) is not tolerance
eligible in practice: off the first overtone's node by 2% of the length f₂ is only 18 dB under f₁.

**What moved.**
- `src/gong-solve.js`: `seatStrike(…, alpha)` and `seatStrikeEnd(…, alpha)` take the tilt (0 is the old
  branch verbatim, bit for bit); `seatStrikeTol` finds the smallest-|α| strike (the exact root first), and
  for an end strike α itself is the root of `g`; `strikeWindow` lists the contacts the budget reaches; the
  solver's lever path (`leverAt`, `leverHammer`, the edge-bar family) tries the untilted blow over every
  standoff first and tilts, 1° at a time, only a run the lever reaches at none, so a seat with an exact root
  keeps it.
- `tools/gong-seats.mjs` carries a result's `alpha` into the seat row (omitted when 0).
- `src/main.js`: the build solves its strike with the row's `alpha` (absent is 0), refuses a row tilted
  past `STRIKE_ALPHA_MAX`, and every consumer already read the blow from `st.b`, so the head, the staff and
  the acoustics follow the tilt.

`spiral`) already enumerate pivots every 2° and blows every 3°, so they gain nothing here; the window is for
the edge-bar family and for a lever that moves. The Gong Bench has no marker for the window yet. The
battery was not run here.
