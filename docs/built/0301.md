## §301 — BrickLink catalogue behind the parts list: purchase ids, colour ids, element availability

**What changed.** §300's parts list marked 36 purchase ids `unverified` and 6
`ldraw-keyword` because BrickLink was unreachable from the sandbox that wrote it.
The owner then downloaded BrickLink's catalogue files (Parts, Colors, Codes,
tab-delimited, from bricklink.com/catalogDownload.asp, 2026-10-20) and BrickLink
became the declared purchase catalogue. `tools/bricklink-subset.mjs` reads the
three downloads and writes `src/technic-bricklink.js`, a generated 51-part,
10-colour subset (the whole files are 14 MB and are not committed). The ids it
reads are `PART_IDS` (`src/technic-bom-ids.js`), which the probe holds equal to
what the builds place. `--check` re-derives the committed file from the downloads
and fails on any difference.

**Measured.**

- **Purchase ids.** All 51 placed ids are now `bricklink` rows. Their numbers agree
  with the nine a person read on 2026-09-12 (`CATALOGUES`) and with all six LDraw
  header keywords (3023b → 3023, 3070b → 3070, 32123a → 4265c, 4032a → 4032,
  4185a → 4185, 22253 → 6595). Where the LDraw id is not a BrickLink item number it
  is an ALTERNATE number of one (22253 and 3070b; the string 75924 is an alternate of
  BrickLink item x127c20pb01, "String with End Studs (20L Overall)", which BrickLink
  also sells as x127c21pb01 — see below), or BrickLink does not list the mould-variant
  letter at all (3023b, 32123a, 4032a, 4185a) and the base item is used, with `via`
  saying so and the line flagged. `unverified` is now 0 rows; the other bases stay in
  `deriveRow` for a part BrickLink does not list.
- **7785 and 7861 are on BrickLink**, each in ONE colour only: 7785 in White (element
  6569662) and 7861 in Light Bluish Gray (6576953). The role table asks for Orange for 7785 (the
  detent wheel), which BrickLink does not list, so that line is flagged; 7861 in Light
  Bluish Gray is listed.
- **Colours.** LDraw 0, 1, 2, 4, 14, 15, 19, 25, 71, 72 map by name to BrickLink 11, 7,
  6, 5, 3, 1, 2, 4, 86, 85. Each line carries BrickLink's colour id and the element
  codes BrickLink lists for that part in that colour.
- **Availability.** Of the 143 lines across the eight 2026 builds, 19 (15 distinct
  (part, colour) pairs) are in a colour BrickLink lists no element for, e.g. 7785
  Orange (BrickLink: White only), 11478 Blue (not listed in Blue), 22253 Dark Bluish
  Gray (Yellow and White only). Those parts count 2 / 9 / 1 / 17 / 4 of 26 / 188 / 73 /
  214 / 45 in rows 4, keyless, fusee, alarm and escapement; rows 1–3 have none. The
  list says so on the line and in the CSV (`bricklink_listed`), and does NOT change
  the colour: the role colours are a design choice (§300), and re-picking them by
  availability, or buying in the listed colour, is the owner's call, filed below.

**Decisions to flip.**

| decision | taken | alternative |
|---|---|---|
| availability gaps | flag the line, keep the role colour | re-pick role colours so every line has a BrickLink element (changes `LDRAW_COLOUR`, the pictures and the .ldr colours) |
| the string | BrickLink item x127c20pb01 (75924 is its alternate number) | BrickLink lists the 20L and 21L strings under one item; LDraw's 75924 is 21 studs/160 mm per `cordChoice`, so the list says "one string" and the length is the model's — confirm the right BrickLink variant by hand |
| committed data | the subset only | commit the 14 MB files (rejected: BrickLink's terms were not read and they would ship in `vendor/`-style payloads) |

**Honesty.** Verified means "matches BrickLink's catalogue download of 2026-10-20",
not "available now": stock, the elements' production status and the 2026 gear's
retail availability are not in these files. Nothing in the model changed; no
placement, matrix or `.ldr` line moved.
