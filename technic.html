The technic fork

The movement's going, motion and reserve trains re-toothed to LEGO Technic's module-1 catalogue. Each chain below multiplies EXACTLY to the ratio the steel train serves, and each shaft turns by the product of the tooth counts before it.

Tooth counts, kinds, centre distances and ratios come from the declared rows. Part shapes, faces and colours are drawn, not LEGO's CAD; beams and frames are not drawn. The 90° meshes on the 60T turntable's bevel top are placed but their teeth are not phased. The escapement, balance and fusee have no LEGO row and are not here.
