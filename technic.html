The technic fork

The movement's going, motion and reserve trains re-toothed to LEGO Technic's module-1 catalogue. Each chain below multiplies EXACTLY to the ratio the steel train serves, and each shaft turns by the product of the tooth counts before it.

Tooth counts, kinds, centre distances and ratios come from the declared rows. In the drawn view, part shapes, faces and colours are drawn, not LEGO's CAD, and every pair's teeth are phased to mesh, including the 90° meshes on the 60T turntable's bevel top. The LEGO build view assembles each row that has a brick frame from the real parts (LDraw.org's library, CC BY 4.0): every dimension of the frame is measured off those parts. Rows on the turntable are not framed yet and stay drawn. The escapement, balance and fusee have no LEGO row and are not here.
