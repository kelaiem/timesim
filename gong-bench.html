The shaded stretch of the scale is roughly where hearing is most sensitive (about 1 to 5 kHz). The marker is f₁.

What is measured and what is not. The free space is read off the real build by tools/gong-bench-data.mjs (generated, never typed; --check re-derives it), at each case's bar seat fit with the round build's ring left out, the same map tools/gong-seats.mjs solves its seats on: every movement triangle within one clearance margin of the wire's z band, on each case kind at its own fitted size. The pitch comes from two bending models of the wire, each a chain of short circular arcs clamped at the foot and free at the struck end: IN PLANE, the model the watch already uses for its gong, extended to any curve; and OUT OF PLANE, the wire flapping across the plate while it twists (Love's curved-rod equations, round section, Poisson's ratio 0.3). The out-of-plane model reproduces Love's free-ring frequencies and a straight bar's bending and twisting roots. Against today's gong (the 61.14° arc at radius 48.2) it reproduces 2093 Hz, the C7 the build tunes to, and it matches a straight bar's textbook roots exactly.

Not modelled here. The hammer is not re-solved: the blow is taken normal to the wire wherever the slider puts it, square in the plate's plane unless you tilt it. A square blow drives only the in-plane modes; a tilt of θ hands sin θ of the impulse to the out-of-plane ones (marked ↕). On a tight curl those can sit below f₁, and two close modes sounding together beat. The foot block's own room is not checked. A spiral's turns must stay apart (pitch at least the wire plus a margin, 3.05 units), which the status line checks. The sound is a sketch. Its partial frequencies come from the model, and so does how strongly a blow at the chosen point excites each one (the mode's own shape there, over its modal mass, as an equal impulse). What the ear finally gets is not modelled: the hammer's contact time and the case's radiation both favour the low partials, so the page rolls the higher partials off by f₁/f and gives each a made-up decay. Strike at about 78% from the foot on a straight bar and its first overtone, sitting on that mode's node, nearly vanishes.
