Watch Sim Translation Review

Watch Sim is a browser simulation of a fusee-and-chain watch movement. Its interface, its plain-language primer and its technical explainer are translated into nineteen languages, all drafted by machine and checked only by software. Software can confirm that numbers and markup survived. It cannot tell whether a sentence reads naturally, or whether a part has the name a watchmaker in your language would use. That is what this review is for.
